# Sakina Account Warm-Up Playbook

> Synthesis of two TikTok account-warm-up playbooks (madebycol UGC-creator playbook + the "5-day new-account" playbook), customized for Sakina's niche. This is the daily protocol for any new Sakina-affiliated TikTok account — founder account, UGC partner, or experimental burner.

## The rules both authors agree on

- **Don't post for the first 48 hours.** The algorithm has zero signal on a new account. Anything posted during this window gets shown to random people, doesn't convert, and trains TikTok to think your content is for the wrong audience.
- **Warm up by behaving like your target customer**, not like a brand. Scroll, save, like, comment, follow — in the FYP, on the niche, for real, 30–60 min/day.
- **Comments must be genuine, never emoji-only or one-word.** TikTok pattern-matches those to bot behavior.
- **5% like rate, not 50%.** Don't like every video — looks like a bot.
- **Follow 1–5 micro-creators per day**, never 50 at once.
- **Repost 1–2 niche videos per day** as part of warm-up.
- **Warm-up compounds daily.** Stop the routine and the account goes cold within days, even after a viral hit. One author had a 2.1M-view account die after 5 days of skipping the routine.


## Pre-launch setup (Day 0, one time)

- **Handle**: prefer `@sakina`, `@sakinaapp`, or branded variant. Lock the same handle on TikTok + IG + YouTube so search across platforms returns us.
- **Profile picture**: Sakina mark on cream `#FBF7F2` or emerald `#1B6B4A`. No founder face yet — keep it brand-led for warm-up.
- **Bio**: one line of brand voice + link. e.g. *"Gentle reflections for a Muslim heart. Free app → link"*. Link points to the app store landing (use a Branch or Linktree if you need multi-destination later).
- **Geo**: set device location to a US city if targeting US-first.
- **Language**: device + TikTok app language = English.

## Days 1–2: passive warm-up (NO POSTING)

Twice a day, 10–30 minutes each. Two sessions matters — algorithm reads session frequency as a humanness signal.

### Search these keywords (rotate across the two days)

| Slot | Keywords |
|---|---|
| Day 1 morning | `dua for anxiety` · `99 names of allah` · `dhikr` |
| Day 1 evening | `muhasabah` · `quran reflection` · `islamic journaling` |
| Day 2 morning | `tawakkul` · `muslim mental health` · `dhul hijjah` |
| Day 2 evening | `islamic affirmations` · `revert muslim` · `dua` |

For each search:
- Watch top videos all the way to the end
- Like the ones that match our voice
- Save the ones whose format we'd want to copy
- Comment on 2–3

### Aggressively tap "Not interested" on

Comedy reposts · auntie-gossip "POV" videos · rizz/dating content · manifesting / law-of-attraction content · astrology · fitness-influencer Muslims · fashion-revert celebrity arcs · sectarian hot-takes. These will appear because they share hashtags with our niche. Train them out hard.

### Comment templates (use as starting points, write your own each time)

- "This was exactly what I needed today, jazakAllah khair."
- "Saved this — going to come back to it tonight."
- "Subhan'Allah, never thought of that ayah this way."
- "The way you broke this down is so calm. Thank you."
- "Sending this to my sister, she'll love this."

Five per day. Different videos. Never the same comment twice — algorithm flags duplicates.

### Repost 1–2 niche videos per day

Mix viral (1M+) with mid-tier (10K–100K) reposts. Different formats within the same niche each time. Always from our follow list or adjacent creators — never from the anti-list.

### Daily Day-1/2 checklist

```
[ ] Two 10-min FYP sessions completed
[ ] 20-30 videos liked from the niche (≈5% like rate)
[ ] 10 videos watched all the way through
[ ] 5 genuine non-emoji comments left
[ ] 2-5 micro-creators followed from the list above
[ ] 1-2 niche videos reposted
[ ] 10 videos saved as future format references
```

### End-of-Day-2 pass/fail check

Open your FYP. **At least 7 of 10 videos should be Islamic/reflective in our niche.**

- **Pass** → proceed to Day 3.
- **Fail** → run another full warm-up day. Posting on a cold account burns the format.

## Warm-up killers (the three things that flatline a new account)

1. Watching a video for 3 seconds and scrolling away repeatedly = negative humanness signal
2. Following 50+ accounts in one session = bot flag
3. Posting before 48 hours = wasted effort

## Day 3: diagnostic slideshows (low-stakes test)

Before risking a real video, post **2 slideshows** as the algorithmic diagnostic. Cream + emerald + gold, Aref Ruqaa + Lora.

Suggested:
1. **"5 Names of Allah for when you feel overwhelmed"** — 5 slides, one Name each, brief meaning
2. **"3 verses for when today feels heavy"** — 3 slides, one ayah each, gold rule beneath

### Pass criteria (check at 5 hours after posting)

```
✅ ≥ 500 impressions
✅ ≥ 20-30 likes
```

- **Both pass** → post up to 3 of your main videos that same day. Use formats from the 2026-05-21 digest (the 12s "If today felt heavy" Sūrat ash-Sharḥ ayah card is the recommended first real post).
- **Either fails** → another full warm-up day. Don't burn the main videos on a cold account.

## Day 3+ retention rule (the only metric that matters once you're posting)

**Target ≥ 42% watch-to-end.**

- Below 42%, TikTok stops the distribution wave; the video dies in its first batch
- Above 42%, TikTok keeps pushing into bigger waves; this is how videos compound to 1M+
- Likes/comments/shares are *secondary* — they're a consequence of retention, not a driver

Practical implications for Sakina's format:
- **First 3 seconds must hook** — use the digest's "If today felt heavy…" pattern, proven
- **Last 3 seconds must earn the watch** — end on the dua reveal or the Name reveal, NOT the CTA
- **No slow middles** — if there's a beat that doesn't add tension or payoff, cut it
- **The Sakina end-card / CTA goes at the very end**, never in the middle — otherwise it breaks the retention curve before the end

## Day 4+: maintenance cadence (THIS is where most accounts die)

Every day, no exception:

```
[ ] 2 videos posted
[ ] 2 × 10-minute FYP sessions
[ ] 5 niche likes
[ ] 2-3 genuine comments
[ ] 1 niche repost
[ ] 1-2 new niche follows (when genuinely found)
```

The single biggest mistake both authors flag: people warm up for 2 days, hit a viral video, then stop the routine. **The account goes cold within days.** Treat the daily routine like brushing teeth.

## Abandon criteria

- **6 consecutive videos under 300 views** → try posting 3 videos recorded NATIVELY inside the TikTok app (no CapCut, no Remotion, no external edits). Sometimes resets the trust signal.
- **Still nothing after those 3** → abandon the account. Start over.

> The warm-up is 2 days. Starting over costs 2 days. That's not a loss. Staying on a dead account for 3 weeks is.

## Multi-account safety (Phase 2, once we have UGC partners)

When we start running multiple Sakina-affiliated creator accounts (per the Cal AI sequencing plan):

- **Do NOT have Sakina-affiliated creators heavily engage with each other** on TikTok.
  - Reason 1: TikTok shows their videos to the same audience pool — wasted reach.
  - Reason 2: If one account gets shadowbanned, the others doing intentional engagement with it can get flagged for suspicious activity.
- Each creator should have an independent FYP profile built via their own warm-up.
- Limit cross-engagement to occasional, organic-looking interactions.

## Verifying a UGC creator partner is actually warming up (madebycol's checklist)

When we bring on paid creators in Phase 2, check their TikTok profile weekly:

- **Following count**: should grow by at least 2–3 micro-creators per day. If 0/day, they're not warming up.
- **Reposts**: should be 2–3 per day, different formats within the niche, mix of viral and mid-tier sources. If 0–4 total reposts on the account, they're not warming up.
- **Comment history**: should be visible on niche videos, genuine and varied. If only their own posts have comments from them, they're not warming up.

If a creator fails these checks, they're not going to break out no matter how good the content is. Fix the warm-up before judging the format.
