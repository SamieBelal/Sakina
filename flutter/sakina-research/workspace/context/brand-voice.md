# Sakina — Brand Voice & Content Filter

This document is the non-negotiable filter for every TikTok trend, hashtag, sound, and creator we consider adapting. If a candidate fails any rule here, it's out — regardless of how many views it has.

## Allowed formats
- **Listicle slideshows** — "5 things to remember when…", "3 verses for when you feel…", "7 Names of Allah for hard days." Static-text-on-cream-background style.
- **Reflective hooks** — slow text-on-image openers that ask the viewer to sit with something. "Before you scroll past this…" / "If today felt heavy…"
- **Names of Allah explainers** — one Name, short meaning, one verse, one practical reflection on when to call on this Name.
- **Gentle Quran reflections** — one ayah, English meaning, a short calm reflection. No dramatic music swells, no shouting.
- **"Things I wish I knew earlier"** — first-person, written-on-text format. Spiritual-growth framing.
- **"POV: you're feeling X"** — ONLY when used reflectively (e.g. "POV: you're overwhelmed and you remember Allah is As-Salam"). Never as a thirst trap, never as ironic Gen-Z cosplay.
- **Quiet visual loops** — Arabic calligraphy reveal, gentle gold-leaf or watercolour animation, single line of text holding the frame.

## Blocked formats
- **Thirst hooks** — "Wait for it…", "You won't believe what happens next…", body-focused or face-zoom hooks, anything optimised for outrage or curiosity-gap manipulation.
- **Gen-Z brainrot** — Skibidi-anything, "delulu," ironic-detachment humour, meme overlays, fast-cut comedy with vocal stings.
- **Sarcastic / edgy comedy** — making fun of other Muslims, other faiths, "POV: when the auntie…" gossip humour, in-group mockery.
- **Sexual or romantic content** — including soft thirst posts, "halal husband/wife goals," wedding fantasy reels.
- **Dating / "rizz" content** — anything about attracting a partner, even framed religiously.
- **Secular pop-psych that conflicts with Islamic ethics** — manifesting, law of attraction, "you are your own god," shadow work framings that treat the self as the highest authority.
- **Astrology** — Mercury retrograde, zodiac, "as a Pisces moon…" — categorically out.
- **The word "manifesting"** and its derivatives. Use "make dua," "trust Allah's plan," "ask Allah."
- **Vocal-heavy music tracks with non-permissible instrumentation** — sounds dominated by strings, brass, drums beyond the daff. When unsure, FLAG don't include. Acceptable: ambient pad sounds with no recognisable instrument, vocal-only nasheed-style audio, nature soundscapes, original spoken word.
- **Trauma-dump or rage-bait** — "things my parents said that broke me" framed for engagement, not healing.
- **Hot takes on scholars, madhabs, or sectarian content** — categorically out.

## Tone rules
- **Reflective** — invite, don't tell. End with a question or a pause, not an answer.
- **Slow** — pacing on the order of seconds per beat, not frames. Whitespace in time, not just on screen.
- **Warm** — cream, gold, soft light. Imagine candlelight, not stadium lights.
- **Intellectually honest** — if a verse has context, respect the context. Don't yank ayahs into self-help slogans.
- **Never preachy** — we don't tell people what to do. We share what helped us.
- **Never performative** — no faux-vulnerability for the algorithm. No "I almost left Islam until…" hook unless it's a real, considered story told carefully.
- **First-person or second-person** — "I" or "you," rarely "we" (avoids the in-group/out-group feel).

## Scoring rubric (apply to every candidate)
For each video / hashtag / sound, score 1–5 on each:
1. **Format fit** — Does it match an allowed format?
2. **Hook tone** — Reflective and gentle, not bait?
3. **Sound ethics** — Does the audio pass the music filter? (FLAG if unsure)
4. **Audience truth** — Would a reflective Muslim 18–35 actually save this, or is it engagement bait?
5. **Adaptability** — Can we remake it in cream/emerald/gold/ink with our typography intact?

A pick must score ≥4 on rules 1, 2, and 4. Rule 3 must be ≥3 or FLAG. Rule 5 must be ≥3.
Anything that fails goes to "Flagged for human review" — never silently dropped.
