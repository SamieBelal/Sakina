## Format insights from this run

1. **Sample size matters more than seed selection.** Bumping `resultsPerPage` from 8 to 20 surfaced 13 brand-safe Tier-1/2 breakouts we missed entirely on the first run. The top-by-views ranking was hiding the FYP-pushed long tail. Going forward `resultsPerPage: 20` is the default in SKILL.md.

2. **The "dead" slideshow format is alive at the V/F level.** @mashsaf12's 10s "Dhul Hijjah to-do list" slideshow at 302× V/F overturns the 2026-05-19 conclusion. Slideshows work — they're just under-supplied. Worth at least one Sakina slideshow per week.

3. **Dhul Hijjah is RIGHT NOW.** Five Tier-1/2 entries are seasonal Dhul Hijjah content (@mashsaf12, @versesinvoice, @mindfulmvslim_, @duas.answered, plus dhikr posts citing Day of Arafah). Eid al-Adha falls in early June this year. **Recommendation: post one Dhul Hijjah-tagged video per day for the next 14 days using the formats below.** Algorithm gives time-bound religious content disproportionate push.

4. **Aggregator-of-scholars accounts are crushing — but we shouldn't copy that model.** @deenmentality (154×) and @knowledgeofislam17 (61×) both ride on reposting Belal Assaad audio. Direct copy is contested; the legitimate version is commissioning our own teacher voice (Format D from the 2026-05-19 digest's content strategy).

5. **One adjacent insight: our top long-form pick last run (@talhaghannam, Quran Reflection Toolkit) is V/F 0.01× on his recent posts** (728 plays from 97K followers). His earlier breakout video that we picked depended on FYP discovery, but his current cadence is purely fan reach. **Implication: long-form scholarly format works for one-off algorithmic hits but isn't a daily-volume play.** Keep it as a weekly Tier-2 release, not the daily backbone.

### Substitutions / quirks (new this run)
- **Sample size beats seed rotation, for now.** Most new breakouts came from hashtags that were in the first run too — they were just past position 8. Until we exhaust the supply of niche-specific hashtags, bumping `resultsPerPage` matters more than rotating seeds.
- **`textLanguage` field is reliable for English filtering** but returns `"un"` (unknown) frequently for Arabic-tagged content even when the visible text is English. When `lang == "un"`, check `text` content directly — if it contains transliterations like "Allah", "tawakkul", "alhamdulillah" but no English sentences, treat as non-English-led.
- **Seasonal hashtag `dhulhijjah` over-delivered.** Added it as a new seed this run; it produced 4 of the Tier-1/2 picks. Worth keeping as a permanent seed during the Dhul Hijjah window (~last week of May through mid-June 2026), then rotating out post-Eid.
