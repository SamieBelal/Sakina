# Sakina TikTok Digest — 2026-05-19

First pipeline run. Phase 1 prototype.

**Quick read of the landscape:** The reflective-Muslim niche on TikTok is dominated by short-form talking-head videos with text overlays and direct emotion→dua/dhikr framings. Slideshows are essentially absent from the top performers, despite being listed as an allowed format in our brand voice — worth a note. Original sound use is overwhelming (≈75% of usable picks); the top US trending sounds list is 100% music-with-instruments (Cinco de Mayo mariachi + commercial pop) and provides nothing for Sakina.

## Format insights

1. **Slideshows are nearly absent from top performers in this niche.** Across all 96 videos pulled, only zero of the top 10 by save count are slideshows. Our brand voice anticipated slideshows as a primary format — the data says short-form voiceover + text overlay is what's working in the reflective-Muslim niche. *Worth a brand-voice update.*

2. **Original sound dominates (≈75% of top picks).** This is good news for brand-safety — we don't need to ride trending sounds (which mostly fail our music filter). It also means our voice and our own ambient soundscapes can become recognisable assets.

3. **Hook structures over-indexing:** (a) direct imperative + Name/dua ("Memorize them. Call upon them."), (b) reply-to-comment framing ("Replying to @… here's the dua…"), (c) scholarly-listicle title-cards ("10 levels of…", "The Daily 3 M's"), (d) reframe questions about pop-psych ("Do we believe in affirmations?").

4. **Save rate is a better Sakina signal than play count.** The two top picks (@mualafcentral, @waveofserenity) have save rates of 3.6–4.4%, which is unusually high. Our audience saves what they'll return to — exactly the behavior our app rewards. Optimize for saves over views.

5. **Voice-only nasheed (Ahmed Bukhatir tracks surfaced twice — "Ya Adheeman", "Waqafa Al Telfo") is the one music category passing the filter cleanly.** Build a small approved-sounds library and reuse.

## Content strategy for today

- **Recommended format:** 25–30s "dua for [emotion]" micro-video. Cream background. Single Aref Ruqaa Arabic line center-frame at 48pt. Lora English transliteration beneath. Voiceover reads the Arabic + English. Identify the Name of Allah being invoked + source citation in small gold caps in the corner. End with "save this for the next time you feel [emotion]."
- **Suggested hook in Sakina's voice:** "If today felt heavy — this is the dua the Prophet ﷺ made when he felt the same."
- **Visual approach:** cream background `#FBF7F2`, emerald text `#1B6B4A` for English, ink-near-black `#1A1A2E` for Arabic, matte gold `#C8985E` for the Name of Allah hero card and the source citation. Geometric pattern at 5% opacity behind. Soft fade-in (300ms ease-out) on the Arabic.
- **Experimental angle to test:** a "Reflection Toolkit" long-form series modeled on @talhaghannam — 2–3 minute deep dives into a single Islamic framework (the 3 M's, the 10 levels of Qur'anic engagement, the four states of tawakkul). Lower view ceiling but builds Sakina's claim to be the *intellectually serious* reflective-Muslim brand. Counter-positions against the manifestation-Muslim crowd. Series episodes also become long-form blog posts and in-app content.

### Substitutions / quirks
- `khadinakbar/tiktok-trending-hashtags-scraper` returned **0 items** with status "⚠️ No data collected — check run logs" despite consuming actor-start credits. Creative Center may be blocking this scraper or the input config needs adjustment (we sent `{country: "US", timePeriod: "7", maxResults: 100}`). For tomorrow's run, either retry with `industry` set, or substitute `parseforge/tiktok-hashtag-analytics-scraper` (different vendor, also targets Creative Center). Our 12 seed hashtags compensated — we caught the niche regardless.
- Apify dataset responses come back with **flat dot-notation keys** (e.g. `"searchHashtag.name"` as a literal string key), not nested objects. `jq` queries need bracket-string notation (`.["searchHashtag.name"]`), not dot navigation. Documented in the SKILL.
- 12-hashtag batch returned **8 videos per hashtag exactly** (the `resultsPerPage` cap), so adjusting that value is the cleanest knob for cost vs. coverage in future runs.
