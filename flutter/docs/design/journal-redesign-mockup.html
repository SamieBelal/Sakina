What changed, and why

  • Metadata leaves the card. Type + date now sit on the background above it, the way the reference floats its date and mood pills. The card face is only content.
  • Your words become the headline. 15px grey italic ellipsis → 19px / w500 / near-black, 3 lines, fading out instead of cutting off. The reference's biggest move is that the card leads with a large, dark line; ours led with an app-generated badge.
  • The Name moves to the footer. Reading order is now what you carried → the Name you met. It was inverted.
  • Bigger, softer cards. Padding 16 → 22, radius 14 → 20, gap 12 → 26. Border and the 4px gold rail are gone: every card had the same rail, so it carried no information — the coloured dot in the meta row does that job in 6px.
  • The 4-tile stats box collapses to one line under the title, and the tab pill becomes an underline row. Together with a smaller top pad that is ~92px reclaimed — the first entry starts at 237px instead of 329px.

Taken from the reference, deliberately not taken

  • The permanent search bar — costs a full 72px row on the first screen for a control most sessions never touch, and we just shipped search as a mode behind the header icon. The icon stays.
  • Mood emoji chips — we have no mood model, and adding one is a product decision, not a layout one. The Name is our equivalent and it is richer.
  • Dashed separators — chrome doing a job whitespace already does.
  • Centred title — every other screen in the app is left-aligned.

The one real constraint on colour

The reference tints its cards because its background is white. Ours is already warm cream (#FBF7F2), so a sand tint nearly vanishes and only the emerald wash reads. B uses white cards, which get their separation from the cream background for free — that is the closer analogue of what the reference is doing. C is there so you can judge the tint against the real background rather than in the abstract.
