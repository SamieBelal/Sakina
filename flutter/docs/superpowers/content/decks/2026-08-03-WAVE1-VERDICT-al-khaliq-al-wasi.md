## 0. THE RULING ASKED FOR FIRST — the Al-Wasi bar-1 enumeration is REFUTED

The deck stakes bar 1 on an enumeration it says came from working outward rather than from a concordance, and nominates it as the first thing to attack. I attacked it with a concordance.

**Method.** All 114 sūrahs fetched from `api.quran.com/api/v4/verses/by_chapter/{n}?fields=text_uthmani` — **6,236 āyāt, the complete muṣḥaf**. Every word containing the consonant sequence س+ع (after folding all combining marks, dagger alif, taṭwīl and alif variants) extracted and classified by hand. 87 āyāt matched; 30 word-tokens are `w-s-ʿ`. This is the concordance query the drafter says it did not run.

### 0a. There is a sixth finite verb. The list of five is not exhaustive.

Finite `w-s-ʿ` verbs in the Qurʾān, complete: **2:255 · 6:80 · 7:89 · 7:156 · 20:98 · 40:7.** That is **six**, not five.

**Qurʾān 7:89** — `وَسِعَ رَبَّنَا كُلَّ شَىْءٍ عِلْمًا` … fetched: the āyah reads `… وَسِعَ رَبُّنَا كُلَّ شَىْءٍ عِلْمًا …`, SI *"Our Lord has encompassed all things in knowledge."* Speaker: **Shuʿayb**, replying to the arrogant chiefs of his people (7:88 names him). It is absent from the deck's table, from its authoring notes, and from its rejection list.

7:89 fails on the same ground as 20:98 and 6:80 — a prophet's speech about Allah — so the *conclusion* of clause (a) survives. **But the claim as written ("all five … and all unavailable") is false, and it is the claim the founder is being asked to sign.**

### 0b. Clause (b)'s taxonomy is refuted outright — 51:47

The deck asserts that everywhere else the root is **a trailing epithet, a predicate of a made thing, or human means.** It is not.

> **Qurʾān 51:47** — `وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بَنَيْنَـٰهَا بِأَيْي۟دٍ وَإِنَّآ لَمُوسِعُونَ`
> SI: *"And the heaven We constructed with strength, and indeed, We are [its] expander."*

`لَمُوسِعُونَ` is a Form-IV `w-s-ʿ` active participle **predicated of Allah Himself, in Allah's own first-person speech, attached to an act He is performing on a thing He made.** It is none of the deck's three categories. It appears **nowhere** in the draft — not in the table, not among the eight epithets, not in the fourteen rejected candidates.

**Why this is not a technicality.** On the letter of bar 1 this is a *stronger* site than 2:115: the Name's own root, Allah as subject, Allah's own voice, an act in progress. And the deck cannot reject it on the grounds it used elsewhere:

- **Bar 5 backwards** — 51:46 is the destruction of Nūḥ's people. But the deck **accepts** backward-punishment adjacency on *both* of its own excerpts (2:114 *"a great punishment"*, 29:55 *"the punishment will cover them"*). 51:47 opens on its own `وَٱلسَّمَآءَ`, exactly the mitigation the deck claims for 2:115's `وَلِلَّهِ`.
- **Bar 5 forwards** — 51:48 (the earth spread out), 51:49 (pairs), 51:50 *"So flee to Allāh… a clear warner."* A warner three āyāt out is milder than what the deck already tolerates one āyah backwards.
- **Availability** — verified free: 51:47 is cited by none of the 24 shipped decks and none of the wave-1 drafts.

It has real hazards (a cosmological register; 51:49 is the sibling `al-khaliq` draft's own runner-up). **Those hazards were never weighed, because the site was never found.**

`6:147` (`رَحْمَةٍ وَٰسِعَةٍ`) · `2:236` (`ٱلْمُوسِعِ`) · `24:22` (`وَٱلسَّعَةِ`) · and the five `وُسْعَهَا` sites `2:233 · 2:286 · 6:152 · 7:42 · 23:62`. Most do fall inside the deck's categories; the point is that the deck did not know they existed while claiming a sweep.

## 1. `al-wasi@1` — the rest

### 1a. Bar 1's fallback, asked strictly: the deck is leaning on the epithet, one remove out

The claim is that bar 1 rests on 2:115's **body** — `فَأَيْنَمَا تُوَلُّوا۟ فَثَمَّ وَجْهُ ٱللَّهِ` — with `وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ` only *labelling* it.

The body demonstrates **ubiquity of Allah's face and ownership of the two extremes of direction.** The deck's own declared distinguishing quality (bar 2) is **capacity / room.** *Ubiquity is not capacity.* The bridge between them is supplied by exactly the two carriers bar 1 forbids by name:

1. **the trailing epithet** `وَٰسِعٌ` — which the deck **keeps on screen** in beat 3 and explicitly declines to cut; and
2. **the deck's own authored prose** — beat 4, *"It says there is no direction that is **outside**."*

The epithet-labels-the-body argument would carry if the body showed capacity. It shows extent of presence. That is the `al-waliyy@1` shape at one remove: the epithet is not asserting the Name directly, it is supplying the *category* the body's content does not itself name. 29:56 does show capacity — of `أَرْضِى`, a made thing, which the deck itself files as unable to carry bar 1.

**And the two problems are one problem:** if the founder takes the deck's own offered one-line fix and truncates beat 3's epithet to clean the Ar-Raḥmān collision, **bar 1 loses its label.** The deck cannot fix the collision and keep the bar.
