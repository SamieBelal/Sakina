# The shared `yā Laṭīf` duʿā on ids 26 / 49 — research, 2026-08-05

**Question put by the founder:** find, for `al-hakeem@1` (26) and `al-khabeer@1` (49),
an authentic narrated supplication that invokes **their own** Name rather than
Al-Lateef's. Leave it alone if nothing genuine exists.

**Answer: one of the two is fixable, the other is not.**

---

## 0 · What the defect actually is — narrower than reported

Both decks carry, byte-for-byte, the catalogue duʿā for ids 26 and 49:

> `اللَّهُمَّ يَا لَطِيفُ الْطُفْ بِي فِي أُمُورِي كُلِّهَا`
> *"O Allah, O Gentle One, be gentle with me in all my affairs."* — `source: ""`

**A shared duʿā is not itself the defect.** 14 duplicate groups exist in
`collectible_names.json`, and most are principled: 24/25 say *"yā Qābiḍu yā
Bāsiṭu"*, 43/44 say *"aʿizzanī … wa lā tudhillanī"*, 79/80 and 81/82 quote the
Muslim 2713 pairing. Those invoke **both** Names of their pair.

The defect at 26/49 is that theirs invokes a **third** Name belonging to neither.
It arose from a real Qurʾānic chain — `لَطِيفٌ خَبِيرٌ` is a fixed pairing (6:103,
22:63, 31:16, 33:34, 67:14), and `حَكِيمٌ خَبِيرٌ` is another (6:18, 11:1) — so
26 inherited 49's partner's duʿā. It is a drift, not an invention.

⚠️ **And it is not unique.** Ids **62 / 63** (`al-qawiyy`, `al-mateen`) share
`لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ` — which invokes
**Al-ʿAliyy (52)** and **Al-ʿAẓīm (50)**, and neither 62 nor 63. That pair has
already shipped. Whatever is decided for 26/49 should be decided for 62/63 too,
or the inconsistency should be stated deliberately.

## 1 · Al-Khabeer (49) — **nothing exists. Leave it.**

**Qurʾān — complete sweep, 43 occurrences of the Name-form, none in duʿā form.**

| query | hits | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `خبير` | 25 | all declarative or 2nd-person address |
| `الخبير` | 6 | 6:18, 6:73, 6:103, 34:1, 66:3, 67:14 — all declarative |
| `خبيرا` | 12 | all declarative (`وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ … خَبِيرًۢا`) |

Not one is a supplication. There is **no `رَبَّنَا …` or vocative form ending in
al-Khabeer anywhere in the Qurʾān.** The root sweep (`corpus?q=xbr`) returns 52
including verb forms, consistent with the above.

**Ḥadīth — the supplication chapters of six collections, swept diacritic-normalised.**

Fetched via Wayback: Bukhārī 75 + 80, Muslim 1 + 48, Tirmidhī 48, Abū Dāwūd 8,
Ibn Mājah 34, Nasāʾī 50.

> ⚠️ **Method note (§9bq).** A first pass returned zero hits for *everything*,
> including `الحكيم`. That was a **tool failure, not a finding** — sunnah.com
> serves fully diacriticised Arabic (`الْحَكِيمُ`), so an undiacriticised pattern
> matches nothing and fails silently in the direction that looks like a clean
> negative. Every count below is from the normalised re-run.

**`يا خبير` — zero occurrences across all eight books.** The only appearances of
`الخبير` at all are inside the **99-Names enumeration** (Tirmidhī 3507 / Ibn Mājah
3861), which is (a) a list, not a prayer, and (b) `Daʿīf` — the enumeration was
already ruled unusable this project.

**Conclusion: there is no authentic narrated supplication invoking Al-Khabeer.**
Not a failure to find one — the sweep is complete on both corpora. The deck keeps
the `yā Laṭīf` duʿā and keeps its honest disclosure. **This is the correct outcome,
not a gap.**

## 2 · Al-Hakeem (26) — **one genuine candidate: Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2696**

Found in Muslim's *Book of Dhikr, Supplication, Repentance and Istighfār*
(Book 48, Ḥadīth 43). A bedouin asks the Prophet ﷺ to **teach him words to say** —
the strongest possible provenance for a duʿā beat, since it is the Prophet ﷺ
explicitly prescribing a supplication.

**Arabic, as fetched:**

> `لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ كَبِيرًا، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ كَثِيرًا، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَزِيزِ الْحَكِيمِ`

**Published translation (sunnah.com, ʿAbdul Ḥamīd Ṣiddīqī):**

> "There is no god but Allah, the One, having no partner with Him. Allah is the
> Greatest of the great and all praise is due to Him. Hallowed be Allah, the Lord
> of the worlds, there is no Might and Power but that of Allah, the All-Powerful
> and the Wise."

**Grade:** Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim — ṣaḥīḥ by collection. ⚠️ **No printed grade line**;
sunnah.com prints grade lines for the Sunan, not for the Ṣaḥīḥayn. Recorded as
collection-level, per the precision correction accepted on 2026-08-04.

**Thematic fit — strong.** `al-hakeem@1`'s move is *"what have you been trying to
work out that you do not actually have the information for?"* The occasion of this
ḥadīth is a man who **could not work out what to say**, being taught. The clause
`لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ` is the surrender of exactly the working-out the deck names.

### Two things wrong with it, stated

1. **It also names Al-ʿAzīz (id 8).** No candidate avoids this — every Qurʾānic
   duʿā-form containing al-Ḥakīm pairs it (2:32 `الْعَلِيمُ الْحَكِيمُ`; 2:129, 5:118,
   60:5 all `الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ`). Al-Ḥakīm does not appear alone in a
   supplication anywhere. A two-Name duʿā is the only form that exists.
2. **Length: 212 characters** against a catalogue median of 70 and a current
   maximum of 125 (`ash-shafi@1`). It would be the longest duʿā in the app by 70%.
   **Needs a device check on `DuaTextBlock` before it ships.**

### The rejected alternative

**Qurʾān 2:32** — `سُبْحَـٰنَكَ لَا عِلْمَ لَنَآ إِلَّا مَا عَلَّمْتَنَآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلْعَلِيمُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ`
*"Exalted are You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us…"*

Thematically it is the **better** text — it is literally about not having the
information. Rejected anyway on two counts: it is **angelic speech**, and §9bk was
ruled this month only for bar 1 (demonstration), leaving its use as a *recited
supplication* an open question the founder has not been asked; and it names
Al-ʿAlīm (14) rather than avoiding the second-Name problem. Muslim 2696 needs no
new ruling.

## 3 · Recommendation

| deck | action |
|---|---|
| `al-khabeer@1` (49) | **Leave.** Nothing authentic exists. Keep the disclosure. |
| `al-hakeem@1` (26) | **Swap to Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2696**, pending the two caveats above. |
| `al-qawiyy@1` / `al-mateen@1` (62/63) | **Founder call** — same defect, already shipped. |

**Not applied.** The swap requires editing `assets/content/collectible_names.json`
(the ship gate asserts the deck's duʿā Arabic equals the catalogue's byte-for-byte),
which is a protected content asset and also changes the collectible card surface.
That is a founder edit, not an agent edit — the research is here so the decision
can be made on evidence rather than on my summary of it.
