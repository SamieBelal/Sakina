### ⚠️ The one reading this deck must not take — recorded so nobody adds it later

**`لَمُوسِعُونَ` has more than one classical reading, and one modern one that must be refused outright.**

- **Saheeh International:** *"We are [its] expander"* — an ongoing act on the heaven.
- **Abdel Haleem:** *"made them vast"* — a completed act. **Both fetched 2026-08-03.**
- **A well-attested classical reading takes `مُوسِعُونَ` from `أَوْسَعَ` in its sense of *having ample means / ample power*** — *"We are possessed of vast ability"* — rather than *"the ones widening it"*.

⚠️ **R2 CLAIMED THE DISPUTE WAS HARMLESS. IT IS NOT, AND THE CORRECTION MATTERS.** R2 wrote that bar 1 *"rests on agency, which every reading carries."* **The verifier fetched 8 published renderings of 51:47 and the split is 6–2: Maududi and Hilali-Khan read `مُوسِعُونَ` as CAPABILITY ONLY** (*"possessed of vast power"*), with no act of widening at all. **So "every reading carries agency" is false on 2 of 8**, and if bar 1 rested only on 51:47 it would fail under those two.

**Bar 1 survives anyway — on 51:48, not on 51:47.** `وَٱلْأَرْضَ فَرَشْنَـٰهَا` is a **finite first-person verb of Allah's act** with no contested morphology: *"And the earth We have spread out."* **All eight renderings carry the making of room there.** 51:47 remains the deck's bar-4 anchor and its most striking line; **51:48 is what makes bar 1 safe.** R2 staked everything on the disputed word and did not notice its own second quotation was dispute-proof.
