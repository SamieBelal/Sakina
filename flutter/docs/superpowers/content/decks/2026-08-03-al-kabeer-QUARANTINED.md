## ⚠️ Read first — the hazards and strategy

**1. Compound epithets with Al-Ali (reserved).** Al-Kabeer appears paired with Al-Ali (The Most High) in both 4:34 ("ٱللَّهُ كَانَ عَلِيًّا كَبِيرًا") and 22:62 ("ٱلْعَلِىُّ ٱلْكَبِيرُ"). Both verses are entirely reserved for Al-Ali (id 52), which has not yet been drafted. This deck **does not touch 4:34 or 22:62**. The strategy is coordinated disengagement: if both Names draft concurrently, no collision from either compound epithet.

**2. Arrogance polemic — the root doing opposite work.** Approximately 15 of the 34 verses containing ك-ب-ر describe human arrogance, refusal, or rebellion against Allah (استكبروا, كِبْرٌ as pride, etc.). These verses use the root as a description of human *vice*, not divine *attribute*. All such verses are rejected on grounds that the root is working against the Name, not for it. Examples rejected: 7:17, 25:21 (عَتَوْ عُتُوًّا كَبِيرًا), 40:72+ (Pharaonic refusal).

**3. The Ibrāhīm irony — nothing people call great stands.** 21:57–64 has Ibrāhīm smash the idols, leaving the greatest one (كَبِيرًا) unbroken, then tell the people: "Ask the greatest one if it did this — if they can speak, ask them!" The irony is devastating: he names the object "greatest" to show its powerlessness. This demonstrates the Name's opposite: what is *called* great by humans is nothing; only Allah's Greatness is real.

### Bar 3 in full — Arabic roots and rendered English

**Roots carried by the quoted text:** ك-ب-ر (21:63 كَبِيرُهُمْ; 13:9 ٱلْكَبِيرُ; duʿā كِبْرٌ in the protective sense); ع-ل-م (13:9 عَـٰلِمُ, Knower — present but not the Name being demonstrated); ع-ل-و (13:9 ٱلْمُتَعَالِ, Most Exalted — present as a paired epithet with Al-Kabeer, both in Allah's narration, both demonstrating supremacy through knowledge).

**Engine (the move — bar-3 surface-c):** The story demonstrates the Name through **exposure of false claims to greatness**. What people cling to as great (idols, power, authority) falls silent when tested. The only Greatness that stands is Allah's, shown through Allah's own knowledge (13:9). This is distinct from:
- `al-azeem@1` (hidden Magnificence carrying one through crisis)
- `al-azeez@1` (Mighty — conferring strength and power; active domination)
- `al-mutakabbir@1` (The Arrogant — though this is a paradoxical Name not commonly discussed; arrogance as divine attribute is a deep topic)

The move here is **truth-exposure** — not crisis-carrying, not power-conferring, but the *revelation that what seemed great has no reality*.

### Ibrāhīm narrative — intentional exclusions

**21:65–71 (the people's recognition and Ibrāhīm's teaching) is quoted nowhere in this deck**, though it is the natural sequel. Why: the story beats (3–5) render only the *demonstration* and *irony* of the moment; the verse beat (13:9) shifts to Allah's own voice. Adding Ibrāhīm's subsequent teaching would be *explaining* the Name rather than *demonstrating* it. The silence of the idols is louder than any sermon.

**27:24 (Āṣif's claim to Sulaymān: I can bring you the throne before you blink) is not used**, though it also plays with the theme of false greatness. Why: it involves Sulaymān's greater power displayed in response (he does bring it faster), which would shift the engine from "false greatness falls" to "true power overcomes false claims." Different engine, different deck-shape. Held free for potential use.

## Pairing and separation

**Against `al-azeem@1` (id 50, drafted concurrently):**

Al-Azeem's story (Uḥud aftermath) emphasizes *magnitude revealed through deliverance* — the hidden scale that was always carrying the believers. The engine is discovery of what was already there.

Al-Kabeer's story (Ibrāhīm and the idols) emphasizes what people *call* great falling to nothing; only Allah's Greatness stands. The engine is *exposure and reversal of false claims*.

**Different narratives, different arcs, different moments of revelation.** The two Names do not collide on story, text, or engine.

**Ship separately or together?** Both decks are self-contained and bar-complete. If Al-Ali (id 52) is also being drafted, **coordinate with it to ensure 4:34 and 22:62 are not touched by this deck** (they are reserved). Otherwise, **ship independently.**
