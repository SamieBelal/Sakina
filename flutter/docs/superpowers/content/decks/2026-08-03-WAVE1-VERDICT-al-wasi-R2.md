### 0a. The morphology — CONFIRMED, independently

`لَمُوسِعُونَ` = emphatic `لَ` + `مُوسِعُونَ`, the **Form-IV (`أَوْسَعَ`) active participle** of `و-س-ع`,
masculine plural. Confirmed two ways rather than asserted:

1. **Pattern.** `مُفْعِل` on a hollow wāw-initial root gives `مُوسِع`; the wāw survives as the long `ū`.
2. **Internal parallel.** The identical form occurs once more in the muṣḥaf — **2:236 `ٱلْمُوسِعِ`**,
   Saheeh International *"the wealthy"*, Pickthall *"the rich"* — of a **human of ample means**.
   This is the load-bearing corroboration and it cuts **both ways**: it proves the form, and it shows
   the form's other Qurʾānic sense is *possessed of ample means*, not *one who widens X*. See §0c.

The deck's Form-II claim for the duʿā also checks: `وَسِّعْ` is the Form-II (`وَسَّعَ`) imperative.
Root-and-form link between the petition and the anchor: **confirmed**.

word is a clause-final **statement of a divine attribute** — which is precisely the shape bar 1
forbids and bar 2 calls *stated, not shown*. The lexical evidence for that minority reading is not
weak: **the only other Qurʾānic instance of this exact form, 2:236, carries exactly that sense.**

**Bar 1 survives anyway, and here is why — the reason is not the one the deck gives.** Even on the
capability readings, **51:48's `فَرَشْنَـٰهَا`** (*"And the earth We have spread out"*) is a finite
first-person divine verb whose semantic content **is** making-room, and it is on beat 4. The
deck's declared quality — *capacity as something made* — is demonstrated by an act in Allah's own
voice under **all eight** renderings. **The deck's own defence is weaker than the defence available
to it**, because it stakes everything on the disputed word and does not notice that its second
quotation carries the quality independently of the dispute.

**And one beat does depend on the disputed reading — ⚠️ FIX 1.** The deck answers *"does any beat
depend on a reading not all three support?"* with no. It is no on beat 3 and **yes on beat 8**:

| beat | rendered string | problem |
|---|---|---|

| 8 | *"He constructs the heaven **and says We are its expander**."* | ⚠️ **the brackets are gone.** A translator's marked interpolation is converted into **unmarked speech attributed to Allah** (*"and says …"*), on a beat that carries no citation. |

1. **This is plan §6 rule 2 verbatim** — *"a deck can adjudicate a contested attribute by choice of

**FIX 1: restore the brackets on beat 8, or reword so the object is not asserted** (e.g. *"He
constructs the heaven and says of it: We are its expander"* still asserts it; *"He constructs the
