## 2 · Why this story, duʿā-first — and why bar 1 moved off Nūḥ's speech

**The shared duʿā** (`اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي رَاضِيًا بِمَا قَسَمْتَ لِي وَبَارِكْ لِي فِيهِ`) is
analysed in full in §6, present verbatim in both drafts, and in `.context/claims/77.md`'s §2. In
short: it carries neither Name's root, has no vocative, and its own root (`q-s-m`) is a contentment-
with-decree prayer that fits either half of this antonym pair equally.

### Why bar 1 rests on Bukhārī 6398, not on Sūrat Nūḥ

R1 built this deck's bar 1 on Qurʾān 71:4, arguing that Nūḥ relaying Allah's offer to his people
("He will forgive you… and delay you…") sits closer to Allah's own words than a human's personal
declaration of faith, because a messenger conveys revealed content rather than testifying about a
private relationship with Allah.

**The coordinator's ruling is correct and this draft adopts it without reservation.** This project
already settled the harder version of this question: `2026-08-03-al-ahad-DRAFT.md` ruled that `قُلْ
هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ` (112:1) **fails bar 1**, because `قُلْ` is Allah *commanding the Prophet ﷺ to
recite* content that is then, once recited, a first-person human declaration about Allah — not Allah
demonstrating an act in Allah's own narrative voice. A `قُلْ` construction is Allah's own direct
speech *dictating the exact words*, which is a stronger claim to divine voice than an unprompted
narration of what a prophet said to his people. **If that fails bar 1, Nūḥ's reported call — never
marked with `قُلْ` at all, and read continuously from 71:3 through 71:4 as one uninterrupted quoted
speech — fails a fortiori.** There is no distinction left to draw. R1's attempted distinction
(messenger-relaying-revelation vs. personal-testimony) does not survive contact with the 112:1
ruling, because both are still, grammatically, *someone other than Allah, speaking* — and 71:4's
`إِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا جَآءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُ` clause is inside that same uninterrupted reported
speech, not a separate narrator-voice sentence — checked directly: 71:3–4 has no intervening `قَالَ`
or shift of grammatical person that would break out of Nūḥ's address.

**So bar 1 for this Name has the same root problem the twin has, and the same resolution.** Qurʾān
71:4, whatever register merits it has, cannot carry bar 1. The only text left that survives is the one
already established as the pair's sole primary attestation: Bukhārī 6398.
