### The one thing this story does not say

**Tirmidhī 2470 does not say the sheep was given in charity.** The Arabic is
`أَنَّهُمْ ذَبَحُوا شَاةً` — "they slaughtered a sheep" — and then the exchange. The near-universal
reading is that it was distributed as ṣadaqa, and that reading is what makes the Prophet's ﷺ answer
legible. **The deck does not assert it.** Beat 3 says only *"Most of it was already gone"* and beat 8
*"the part they kept"* — both entailed by ʿĀʾisha's own sentence. **No beat names charity, a
recipient, or a destination.**

**R2 — and this is where revision 1 actually failed, so it is worth being precise about what changed.**
R1 wrote *"Most of it **left the house**"* and *"the part that **never left**"* and defended both as
entailments. **They are not.** `مَا بَقِيَ مِنْهَا إِلاَّ كَتِفُهَا` says nothing about *location*:
it is equally satisfied if the household ate the meat. R1 had guarded the destination (*charity*) and
then walked past the **direction** — a smaller, quieter unsourced fact of the same kind, in the same
sentence, while writing a section titled *"the one thing this story does not say"*. **"Was already
gone" and "they kept" are strictly weaker and strictly entailed**, and the beat loses nothing.

The premise is instead supplied by **beat 6**, which arrives immediately after and states the rule in
Allah's words. **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2958a** was fetched as corroboration for the same principle —
*"…is there anything as your belonging except that which you consumed… or which you gave as charity
and sent it forward?"*, Arabic `أَوْ تَصَدَّقْتَ فَأَمْضَيْتَ` — but it is **quoted nowhere** and no
beat depends on it. It is in the sources table so the founder can see that the reading the story
relies on has an explicit ṣaḥīḥ statement behind it, not so that the deck can borrow its content.

**Beat 8's middle sentence, *"He counted what was with Allah", is an authored join**, in the same
class as `ar-raheem@1`'s disclosed join between 33:43 and the Kahf story. Neither source puts those
words in the Prophet's ﷺ mouth; the deck is connecting his answer to 16:96, which the reader has just
read on the preceding beat. **One-line alternative if the founder wants zero authored join:**
*"He counted by the rule the āyah just gave."*

## 55:26–27 — the refusal, re-argued honestly, with the escape revision 1 never examined

**This is the section a founder should read before signing, because revision 1 refused this Name's
natural verse on three grounds, one of which was false, and never asked whether its own standard tool
dissolved the other one.**

**1. Bar 1 is stronger on 55:27 than on 16:96, and revision 1 did not say so.**

| | 16:96 (chosen) | 55:27 (refused) |
|---|---|---|
| the clause | `وَمَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ بَاقٍ` | `وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ` |
| `b-q-y` present | yes, as a **participle** | yes, as a **finite verb** |
| what the root predicates | **`مَا عِندَ ٱللَّهِ` — "what is with Allah". Not Allah.** | **`وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ` — your Lord** |

**By the same grammar that awarded 4:130 to Al-Mughni rather than Al-Wasi, my bar-1 row is weaker
than revision 1 stated.** 16:96 gives no finite verb of Allah's own action; it predicates lasting of
*what is with Him*. The bar-1 row below is corrected accordingly. **A reviewer should attack that row
first, not this one.**

**2. The escape was available and I did not examine it.** This deck's own standard instrument — a
**visible ellipsis** — defeats ground (a) completely:

> Everyone upon it will perish, and there will remain the Face of your Lord…

That renders **zero words** of *"Owner of Majesty and Honor"* and leaves 55:28 **outside the
excerpt**, so ground (c) never arises either. Revision 1 applied ellipses to 16:96 and to three other
citations and **never tried the same move on the verse it was refusing.** That is a real gap in the
reasoning and it is recorded rather than quietly closed.

**3. Why I still prefer 16:96 — and the blind verifier independently agrees, so this is a
convergence, not a defence of my first answer.**

- **Bar 2 is the decider, and it runs the other way.** *"Everyone upon it will perish, and there will
  remain the Face of your Lord"* **states** the Name's content outright. That is §9j's ground 1 and
  the `al-haleem@1` rev-1 failure. **16:96 states a rule about two accounts and leaves the showing to
  the story** — which is exactly what beats 4–5 do.
- **The engine needs an antithesis, not a sequence.** The deck's mechanism is a two-column count.
  16:96's `يَنفَدُ` / `بَاقٍ` is that count in one breath. 55:26–27 is perishing *then* remaining —
  a sequence, and the sheep's reversal has nothing to lock onto.
- **The ellipsis is heavier than it looks.** Cutting after `وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ` truncates a construct
  phrase **mid-apposition**; 16:96's ellipsis drops a **complete parallel clause**. Not
  disqualifying, but not free either.

**Net: the refusal survives, on grounds (a) and (c) plus bar 2 — and the founder now has the full
cost of it, including the one axis on which the refused verse is the better text.** 55:26–27 remains
**unspent and free**, ideally for id 89, whose own Name closes 55:27.
