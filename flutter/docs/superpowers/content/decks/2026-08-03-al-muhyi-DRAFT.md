**This is catalogue-locked and this deck cannot fix it** — same class as the *Restorer* collision.
It is reported here as a **new finding**, not actioned (per the ledger's standing rule against
proposing catalogue edits without independent re-verification). What this deck *can* do, and does:

collision is confined to the one catalogue-locked screen (both decks' `dua` beats) rather than
compounded by this deck's own prose. **This deck's engine is evidentiary** ("the proof you can
already see, extended"), not "your heart is like dead ground" — a different move from what id 59's
duʿā already commits that future deck to.

**Diff against `al-qadir@1`** (2:260, Ibrāhīm/birds — *capability* to revive) and `al-muid@1`
(30:27, re-creation — *cyclical restoration*): both read in full. Neither renders `لَمُحْىِ` or any
form of `ح-ي-ي` as their own Name's carrier (their roots are `q-d-r` and `ʿ-w-d` respectively). This
deck's engine — an ordinary, repeatable, witnessed natural process taken as a standing proof — is
distinct from both: `al-qadir@1` asks "can He?" (answered by a miracle, birds returning to a man who
called them from four hills); `al-muid@1` asks "will what ended come back?" (answered by a woman's

*to* the earth, *to* the dead). No story or verse overlap with their candidate material (Abū Bakr's
address after the Prophet's ﷺ death / 40:65).

Unlike Al-Mumeet (which required a full-text sweep to justify trading away the causative verb form
of its root), **this deck needs no bar-4 trade**: `لَمُحْىِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ` in both 30:50 and 41:39 is
the Name's own root, as the ism-fā'il, in a clause with Allah as the subject — bar 1 and bar 4 are
met by the same phrase, twice, independently. The only editorial decision was **trimming the
trailing `قَدِيرٌ` clause from both citations** (§3a) — a bar-3 hygiene choice, not a bar-4
necessity.

## 8 · Method's limits — stated plainly

1. **No independent tafsīr consulted.** The reading of 30:48–50 (nature as a repeatable proof of a
   larger claim) is the plain sense of the passage's own rhetorical structure ("So observe…
   Indeed, that same one…"), not checked against a tafsīr corpus.
2. **The duʿā-provenance search was two queries, not an exhaustive sweep** of sunnah.com, Shamela,
   or Dorar. "No locatable source" is reported as a search result, not a proof of non-existence —
   matching the ledger's standing caution (§7's own note that duʿā narration status is unverified
   project-wide unless independently fetched).
3. **This ledger's own §2a records that all 14 originally-shipped decks predate the successor
   sweep.** This draft's sweep only covers its own six citations; it does not retro-sweep anything
   shipped.
4. **The full-text Qur'an sweep used a local regex over API-fetched `text_uthmani`, not a published
   concordance.** Diacritic-stripping was applied by a single regex pass; a second independent
   method (e.g. a morphological tagger) was not run. The result (16/16 paired) is stated as what
   this method found, not as an infallible enumeration — the same caveat §9ac and §9af record for
   their own full-text sweeps.
5. **No isnād was audited for Bukhārī/Tirmidhī material** — n/a to this specific deck (it carries
   no ḥadīth), but stated for consistency with the plan's standing limit.
