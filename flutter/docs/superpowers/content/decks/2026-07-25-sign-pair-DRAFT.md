**story — The mother among the captives (Sahih al-Bukhari 5999):**

> 2. When she found her own child, she took him to her chest and nursed him. The Prophet ﷺ asked his companions: do you think this woman could throw her son into a fire? Never, they said — not if she could help it.

## Deck `al-lateef@1` — Name₂ *(the "next Name of your journey" tease-copy lives on Name₁'s takeaway, per the sealed-deck spec)*

**bridge:** You couldn't put it into words. The One this Name belongs to never needed them.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 36, verbatim)*: اللَّطِيفُ — Al-Lateef — The Subtle
**story — Yusuf's answer at the end (Qur'an 12:100):**
> 1. Betrayed by his brothers, thrown in a well, sold, imprisoned for years on a false charge — and for most of Yusuf's story, nothing looks rescued.
> 2. At the end — his family gathered, the years explained — he names what had been happening the whole time: "Indeed my Lord is subtle in fulfilling what He wills."
> 3. Not power. Not vindication. Subtle kindness, threaded through every wound — visible only from the far side.
**verse:** "Allah is Ever Kind to His servants." — Qur'an 42:19 (the Name in-text: لَطِيفٌ بِعِبَادِهِ)
**dua** *(catalog 36, verbatim)*: يَا لَطِيفُ الْطُفْ بِي فِيمَا جَرَتْ بِهِ الْمَقَادِيرُ · *Ya Lateefu ultuf bi fima jarat bihi al-maqadir* · "O Subtle One, be gentle with me in all that destiny has decreed."
**takeaway:** "Whether you speak secretly or openly — He surely knows best what is hidden in the heart." (Qur'an 67:13) What you couldn't say was never unsaid to Him.
