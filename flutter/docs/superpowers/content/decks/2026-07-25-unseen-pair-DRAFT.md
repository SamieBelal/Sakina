## Deck `al-baseer@1` — Name₁

**bridge:** You said no one sees it. This Name is about the One who always has.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 46, verbatim)*: الْبَصِيرُ — Al-Baseer — The All-Seeing
**story — Hajar in the empty valley (Sahih al-Bukhari 3364):**
> 1. Ibrahim left Hajar and her baby in a valley with no people and no water. She asked one question: has Allah ordered this? Yes. "Then He will not neglect us."
> 2. When the water ran out, she ran between Safa and Marwa seven times, searching for anyone — "she might see somebody, but she could not see anybody."
> 3. No human eye saw her. But an angel struck the ground — and water broke out: Zamzam — and her running became a rite every pilgrim repeats to this day.
**verse** *(excerpt, marked)*: "…Surely Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing." — Qur'an 58:1 (revealed for a woman whose complaint the person in the same room could not hear)
**dua** *(catalog 46, verbatim)*: يَا بَصِيرُ أَنْتَ تَرَى مَا لَا يَرَى أَحَدٌ فَاشْهَدْ لِي بِمَا لَا يَعْلَمُهُ سِوَاكَ · *Ya Basir, anta tara ma la yara ahad, fashhadli bima la ya'lamuhu siwak* · "O All-Seeing, You see what no one else sees. Bear witness for me in what only You know."
**takeaway (pair synergy):** Al-Baseer sees what you carry. As-Samad — the second Name of your answer — is the One you can set it down on.

**Sources:** [Bukhari 3364](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3364) (sahih, verified via archive — beat 1 quotes Hajar's "Then He will not neglect us"; the angel's later "Allah never neglects His people" is a distinct line in the same hadith, not rendered in the deck) · [Qur'an 58:1](https://quran.com/58/1) ✅ (Name in-text; al-Mujadilah context verified via [Ibn Majah 188](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:188), sahih)

## Deck `as-samad@1` — Name₂

**bridge:** Carrying it alone is the heaviest version. This Name means no one has to.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 34, verbatim)*: الصَّمَدُ — As-Samad — The Eternal Refuge
**story — Zakariyya's hidden call (Qur'an 19:2-7):**
> 1. An old man with no heir prayed a prayer the Qur'an calls private — a hidden call. No audience. No witnesses. Only a lifetime of asking.
> 2. "My Lord! Surely my bones have become brittle, and grey hair has spread across my head, but I have never been disappointed in my prayer to You, my Lord!"
> 3. The answer came with a name no human had ever carried: "We give you the good news of a son, whose name will be John — a name We have not given to anyone before."
**verse:** "Allah—the Sustainer ˹needed by all˺." — Qur'an 112:2 (the Name itself: اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ)
**dua** *(catalog 34, verbatim)*: اللَّهُمَّ يَا صَمَدُ اجْعَلْنِي غَنِيًّا بِكَ عَنْ سِوَاكَ · *Allahumma ya Samad, ij'alni ghaniyyan bika 'an siwak* · "O Allah, O Eternal Refuge, make me needless of all others through You."
**takeaway:** "Needed by all" means everyone leans here — the strongest people you know included. Leaning is not weakness; it is the meaning of the Name.

**Sources:** [Qur'an 19:2](https://quran.com/19/2), [19:3](https://quran.com/19/3), [19:4](https://quran.com/19/4), [19:7](https://quran.com/19/7) ✅ · [112:2](https://quran.com/112/2) ✅ (quran.com renders As-Samad "the Sustainer ˹needed by all˺")
