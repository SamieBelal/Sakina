## Deck `al-mughni@1` — Al-Mughni

**Why this deck exists, in one line:** the user watching other people get the thing, doing the arithmetic, and coming out short. **There is an afternoon in which a whole group of people did exactly that, said it where it got back to the Prophet ﷺ, and were not told a share was coming — they were told what they were already taking home.**

**This Name is *sufficiency*, not supply.** Al-Mughni is what removes the need; Ar-Razzāq — **shipped** — is what arrives. Every selection decision below was made on that line.

> 3. When the day of Hunayn was over, what Allah had granted His Messenger ﷺ was divided among the people whose hearts had recently been reconciled. The Ansar were given nothing of it. The narration says it was as though they found something in themselves, because what reached the others had not reached them.

> 5. He kept going, and then: **"…Are you content that the people go away with the sheep and the camel, and you go away with the Prophet ﷺ to your homes?…"**

- **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2577 (the needle and the sea).** The most famous enrichment ḥadīth in the corpus. **Deliberately not spent:** its insight is shipped `al-kareem@1`'s beat 8 in substance — *the asking costs Him nothing.* **Left free, but a future drafter should know it is effectively pre-spent by an engine, not by a citation.**
- **Tirmidhī 2466 / Ibn Mājah 4107 — *"O son of Adam, devote yourself to My worship and I will fill your chest with richness…"*** **Not used, and the reason matters:** the commonly-circulated English is a **blend of the two collections' wordings**, neither is in Bukhārī or Muslim, and the narration's own second half is a warning clause that a comfort deck would have to elide. **A drafter who wants it must fetch one collection, pick that collection's wording, quote the conditional whole or show the elision, and grade it honestly as ḥasan.** This deck did none of that because it did not need it. **Recorded as unspent and hazardous.**
- **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3464 (the leper, the bald man and the blind man).** Contains `وَكُنْتُ فَقِيرًا فَأَغْنَانِي اللَّهُ` — the Name's root, transitive — and is one of the most vivid narratives in the collection. **Rejected on bar 5 and register:** two of the three are told *"if you are lying, may Allah return you to what you were"*, and the narration closes on Allah's displeasure with them. The punishment is **inside** the excerpt, not merely after it.
- **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 3778 and 4337 (the Anṣār at Ḥunayn, Anas's versions).** **Both fetched.** 3778 renders *"our swords are still dribbling with the blood of Quraysh"*; 4337 narrates the rout and the flight. **Both rejected on register in favour of 4330**, whose text contains no fighting at all. **Also recorded: neither contains the weeping** (*"until their beards were wet"*) that circulates with this story — that is in a Muslim narration this pass **did not fetch**, so **the deck does not claim it and no beat mentions it.** The claim file, which had asserted the weeping and the wrong narrator, is corrected.
- **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 6446 / 1469 (*"richness is the richness of the soul"*, and Ḥakīm b. Ḥizām).** 6446 fetched and used as corroboration only. Both are **prophetic definition or human restraint**, not Allah enriching — they state what this deck's story shows.
- **Qurʾān 59:9 (*"they find no want in their breasts for what the others were given"*).** The most thematically perfect āyah for this exact story — it is **about the Anṣār** — and **rejected on bar 1**: it describes what the Anṣār were, with the divine agent only implied in a passive (`وَمَن يُوقَ`). Bar 1 is the one bar that is not tradeable. **Recorded as a strong beat-8 alternative if the founder wants a Qurʾānic echo of the story that does not carry the verse beat's weight.**
- **Register check:** no beat attributes waiting, wanting or withholding to the Name. Beat 8 makes **no promise to the reader** — no share is said to be coming, in this life or another. The catalogue's `lesson` line for id 93 (*"True richness is when Al-Mughni fills your heart, not just your hands"*) was deliberately **not** used as the takeaway, because it **states** the deck's point and the story shows it.
- **Method limit, stated because the founder signs against this table:** ḥadīth verification here is **not independent of sunnah.com as a corpus** — sunnah.com 403s automated fetching, so Bukhārī 4330, 6446, 3778 and 4337 were all read from Wayback captures of the exact bare-numbered sunnah.com URLs, all deriving from one digitisation. **No printed edition, no Arabic-primary database (Shamela, Dorar), and no isnād audit.** Published grade lines were accepted as printed. Every prior pass in this project has had the same limit.
