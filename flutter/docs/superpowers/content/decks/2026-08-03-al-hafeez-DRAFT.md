## 5. Three-Surface Bar-3 Separation Argument (Four-Way Family)

**These four Names share the protection/watchfulness genre:**

- **Al-Muhaymin** (concurrent): The awake eye over the sleeper. Engine: divine consciousness in your unconscious hours.
- **Al-Hafeez** (this deck): Active preservation of those beyond your reach. Engine: distributed care for loved ones you cannot personally protect.
- **Ar-Raqeeb** (shipped 2026-08-03): The watchers report your prayer unseen. Engine: the report of your unseen good reaches Allah.
- **Al-Mumin** (shipped): Safety is granted not achieved. Engine: protection as a given condition, not a state you strive to maintain.

**(a) Arabic roots:**  
- Al-Muhaymin: ه-ي-م-ن only in verse (59:23, trailing)
- Al-Hafeez: ح-ف-ظ in verse (34:21, finite verb "keeps")
- Ar-Raqeeb: ر-ق-ب (duʿā only, no Qur'ān; documented bar-4 trade)
- Al-Mumin: أ-م-ن (Quran 106:4 finite verb "made safe")

Four distinct roots. No overlap. **(a) PASS**

- Hand/Hands: Al-Hafeez (reaching hand); Ar-Raqeeb (not present); no collision

No 3-word run collision detected. **(b) PASS — clean surface**
