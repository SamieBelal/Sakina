**Beat 1 · bridge:**

**Beat 2 · name_intro** *(from `collectible_names.json` id 14, verbatim)*:
> الْعَلِيمُ — Al-Aleem — The All-Knowing

**Beats 3–5 · story — "What she thought she had delivered":**
> 3. Before her child was born, the wife of ʿImran promised it to Allah's service: **"My Lord, indeed I have pledged to You what is in my womb, consecrated [for Your service], so accept this from me…"**
> 4. The child was a girl. She said: **"My Lord, I have delivered a female."** The ayah answers inside her own sentence: **And Allah was most knowing of what she delivered…**
> 5. Then her sentence resumes, on the other side of it: **"And I have named her Mary, and I seek refuge for her in You and [for] her descendants…"** And the next ayah: **"So her Lord accepted her with good acceptance and caused her to grow in a good manner…"**

> *Allahumma 'Alimal-ghaybi wash-shahadah, Fatiras-samawati wal-ard*
> "O Allah, Knower of the unseen and the seen, Originator of the heavens and the earth."
> **source (proposed): `Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3392 (opening of the supplication)`** — see the duʿā section below. **The founder may equally choose to ship it unpinned; the deck is correct either way.**

**Beat 8 · takeaway:**

### The duʿā — a two-route finding, and an explicit refusal to recommend a catalogue change

**Catalogue id 14's `dua_arabic` is not an authored invocation. It is the opening of a graded narration — and it is the *Tirmidhī* route, not the Abū Dāwūd one.** Both were fetched, because taking only one would have produced exactly the error this pipeline has now made in both prior batches.

| route | fetched text (opening) | grading as printed | clause order |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 3392** (Abū Hurayra ← Abū Bakr) | `قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ عَالِمَ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ فَاطِرَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ رَبَّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَمَلِيكَهُ…` | **ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ** — the page carries Abū ʿĪsā's own words `هَذَا حَدِيثٌ حَسَنٌ صَحِيحٌ` | **`ʿālim` first, then `fāṭir` — the catalogue's order** |
| **Sunan Abī Dāwūd 5067** (same companion chain) | `قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ فَاطِرَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ عَالِمَ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ رَبَّ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ وَمَلِيكَهُ…` | **ṣaḥīḥ (al-Albānī)**, printed on the page | **reversed** |

**Computed, not eyeballed** (`unicodedata` skeleton comparison, diacritics folded): the catalogue string and the Tirmidhī string differ in **exactly one word** — `السَّمَاوَاتِ` vs `السَّمَوَاتِ`, the plene vs. defective spelling of *samāwāt*. Same word, same root, same pronunciation, standard modern orthography. Word order, word count and every other token: identical. The Abū Dāwūd skeleton differs in **clause order**.

**Therefore, and this is the load-bearing sentence: a drafter who had fetched only Abū Dāwūd 5067 would have reported that the catalogue "has the clauses backwards" and recommended reordering id 14 — and that recommendation would have been wrong.** That is the id-51 and id-16 failure shape, third time, caught only by fetching both routes. **This deck recommends NO change to `collectible_names.json`.** The catalogue is right.

**Two honest limits on the pin:**
1. The catalogue text is a **truncation** — it stops after *"and the earth"*, dropping `رَبَّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَمَلِيكَهُ أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ نَفْسِي…`. The ship gate asserts `dua` beats byte-identical to the catalogue, so **an ellipsis cannot be added to the Arabic**. The proposed rendered source string therefore carries the disclosure itself: **`Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3392 (opening of the supplication)`**.
2. `renderedDuaSources` is asserted **bidirectionally**. If the founder approves the pin, **transcription must add `'al-aleem@1': "Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3392 (opening of the supplication)"` to `test/content/name_stories_ship_gate_test.dart`** in the same change; if he declines, **the duʿā beat's `source` must be empty and the deck must NOT appear in that map**. Either is a passing deck. **Do not do half of it.**
