## The tone line this deck is built to hold

**Watchfulness passages sit next to accountability, and this Name's failure mode is making a user feel surveilled at 11pm.** Two structural decisions follow, and they are not stylistic:

1. **The deck never renders a ledger, a record, a reckoning or a count.** The one Qurʾānic āyah that uses this Name's own word in that register — 50:18, `رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدٌ`, *"an observer prepared [to record]"* — was fetched and **refused**, and its `رَقِيب` is an angel, not Allah.
2. **The deck states the distinction out loud on beat 1** — *"This Name is not about being observed. It is about not being left unattended."* — because a user should not have to infer it from tone. **R3 note:** R2 carried this on **beat 8** as well; beat 8 has been rewritten and now *demonstrates* the distinction instead of restating it — the report is given while the people it concerns are absent, which is what *watched over* means and what *watched* does not. **Stating it once, at the front, and showing it at the end is the better arrangement**, and it removed a structural echo of shipped `al-ghafur@1` at the same time.

**And against the two Names it collapses into:**
- **Shipped `al-baseer@1` (The All-Seeing)** is about **being seen in what you carry** (its beat 8: *"Al-Baseer sees what you carry"*; its story is Hājar, *"No human eye saw her"*). This deck contains **no** claim that anything is seen, no claim about a hidden struggle, and no form of `b-ṣ-r`. Its subject is **an unbroken watch and a question asked about you**.
- **Al-Aleem (14), drafting concurrently this wave**, is about being **known**. Its claim file at `.context/claims/14.md` explicitly cedes *watching / seen / recorded* vocabulary to this deck; in return this deck uses knowledge vocabulary on **exactly one clause**, and only to say that knowing is **not** what the asking is for.

## Deck `ar-raqeeb@1` — Ar-Raqeeb

**Why this deck exists, in one line:** the user is awake in the hours nobody else is around for, and assumes those hours go unattended. The narration has two shifts of angels succeeding one another so that no hour does — and then, after they have gone up and out of anyone's reach, **the people it all concerns are spoken about in their absence, and the whole of what is said about them is that they were praying.**

## Bar 4 is TRADED. Here is the sweep that forces it.

**The Name-noun `رَقِيب` occurs five times in the Qurʾān. All five were fetched, in full, 2026-08-03. Not one is usable.**

*Enumeration method, so the claim is checkable rather than recalled:* `api.quran.com/api/v4/search` is **surface-form sensitive**, so it was run three times, once per attested form — `رقيب` → **2** hits (50:18, 11:93) · `رقيبا` → **2** (4:1, 33:52) · `الرقيب` → **1** (5:117). **Union = exactly the five below, and no sixth.** *Limit of the method, stated:* this enumerates the **noun**, not the root. Verbal derivatives of `r-q-b` (`ٱرْتَقِبْ`, `يَرْقُبُوا۟`, `مُرْتَقِبُونَ`) exist and were **not** enumerated — none is the Name, and the one that appears beside a `رَقِيب` (11:93's `وَٱرْتَقِبُوٓا۟`) is refused with the āyah.
