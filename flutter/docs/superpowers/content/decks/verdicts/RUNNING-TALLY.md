### SEND BACK — fixable (1)

**`al-hakam@1` (47).** Carrier 22:56 is sandwiched by punishment on both sides — 22:55 `عَذَابُ يَوْمٍ عَقِيمٍ`, 22:57 `عَذَابٌ مُّهِينٌ`. **Two candidates the drafters never checked, both clean on both sides**, found by the verifier and re-fetched independently by the coordinator:

| candidate | n−1 | n+1 |
|---|---|---|
| **22:69** `ٱللَّهُ يَحْكُمُ بَيْنَكُمْ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ` | ✅ 22:68 `قُلْ`-framed | ✅ 22:70 knowledge + the Record |
| **16:124** `وَإِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَيَحْكُمُ بَيْنَهُمْ` | ✅ 16:123 Ibrāhīm's religion | ✅ 16:125 *"invite… with wisdom"* |

**Coordinator's recommendation: 22:69.** The verifier named the bar-2 cost correctly — both alternatives are bare declarative promises where 22:56 continues into a narrated outcome. But **22:69 is second person, `بَيْنَكُمْ` — "between *you*"**, against 22:56's third-person `بَيْنَهُمْ`. This deck's engine is *the reader with a case nobody will hear*; second person puts that reader inside the sentence and recovers most of the bar-2 loss by a different route.

⚠️ **If 16:124 is chosen instead:** 16:125 contains `بِٱلْحِكْمَةِ` — **Al-Hakeem's (id 26) root** — and must not be rendered.

### REFUSE (1)

**`al-majeed@1` (58).** Bar 1, no route remaining.

**The §9bk ladder is now ruled: angelic speech inside divine narration does NOT carry bar 1.** The deck argued 11:73's `قَالُوٓا۟ … إِنَّهُۥ حَمِيدٌ مَّجِيدٌ` was nearer to *"Allah quoting Himself"* because Allah chose to narrate it. **The refutation: that proves too much** — Allah narrates a prophet's reported speech too, and that rung is already ruled out. The ladder's principle is who is speaking, and the speakers are the messengers.

Routes closed on evidence, not assumption: root sweep independently re-confirmed **complete at 4 occurrences** (`corpus?q=mjd`); the unexplored ḥadīth route was searched and **Tirmidhī 3507 does contain `الْمَجِيدُ` but is `Daʿīf` and is itself a prophet's reported enumeration.**

**Merge question ruled: keep 58 and 72 as two decks.** Classical enumerations list them separately. The cost is an honest catalogue gap on one Name, which is not grounds to paper over a shortfall the text does not force.

**This would be the project's fourth refusal and the first not overturned. It needs a founder decision.**

## Coordinator audit — off-beat citations, all 54 drafts (2026-08-04)

**Motivated by the `al-musawwir@1` finding.** Both prior passes checked only quotations that *render*: the blind verifier's stated scope was the three rendered beats, and R2's was rendered quoted spans. **Nobody had fetched the citations that exist only to justify a bar-5 verdict** — which is where the fabrication was.

**Method.** Every line in all 54 drafts, excluding beat blockquotes, matching the shape `<āyah key>: "<English quotation ≥6 words>"` — the exact shape the defect took. **21 such claims exist**; all 21 fetched and diffed against their cited āyah.

**Result: the fabrication is confined to one deck, and it is worse there than reported.**

**The 40:65 row was marked ✅ by the blind verifier**, which had it as *"matches fetched 40:65 exactly."* It does not. **A verification error inside the verification** — recorded because it is the same class of defect the pass existed to find, and it shows a single independent check is not sufficient on this surface.

**Consequence unchanged in kind, larger in degree:** the deck's `Bar 5: clean` verdict rests on a table that is 5/6 unfounded, and its method-limits section reasons at length about a "cattle simile" that does not exist. **Bar 5 must be re-run from live fetches before this deck ships.** The three rendered beats (3:6, 40:64, 82:8) and the 19-occurrence root sweep are independently confirmed solid and need no rework.
