## 0 · THE RULING ASKED FOR FIRST — `al-haqq@1` bar 5

**BAR 5 IS MET. The deck is not rejected on bar 5.**

This is my ruling from the fetched text, not an acceptance of the drafter's argument. What I fetched
(`api.quran.com/api/v4`, 2026-08-03):

- **20:71** — successor to the last story beat: `فَلَأُقَطِّعَنَّ أَيْدِيَكُمْ وَأَرْجُلَكُم مِّنْ خِلَـٰفٍ وَلَأُصَلِّبَنَّكُمْ فِى جُذُوعِ ٱلنَّخْلِ`
  — *"So I will surely cut off your hands and your feet on opposite sides, and I will crucify you on
  the trunks of palm trees, and you will surely know which of us is more severe in [giving]
  punishment and more enduring."* **Verified verbatim.** The threat is real and it is one āyah away.
- **20:72–73** — the passage's own resolution: the magicians refuse (*"So decree whatever you are to
  decree. You can only decree for this worldly life"*) and it closes `وَٱللَّهُ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ`,
  *"And Allāh is better and more enduring."*
- **7:119** — successor to the verse beat: *"And they [i.e., Pharaoh and his people] were overcome
  right there and became debased."* Not punishment; a defeat, and of Pharaoh's side.
- **7:123–124** — the al-Aʿrāf parallel threat, **six** āyāt beyond the verse beat.
- **7:125–126** — the answer, closing `رَبَّنَآ أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسْلِمِينَ`.

**The three sweep questions, answered from the text:**

1. *Does the successor contradict what the beat asserts?* **No.** 20:71 presupposes the prostration —
   it is a tyrant reacting to it.
2. *Does it complete a thought the excerpt leaves misleadingly open?* **No.** Beat 5 asserts the
   magicians believed. 20:71–73 is evidence that they did, at cost. Nothing half-said is left to
   mislead.
3. *Does the excerpt stop short of the passage's own ending in a way that changes its meaning?*
   **No — and this is the decisive one.** The excerpt stops at 20:70, the passage's climax. The
   passage's own ending, 20:72–73, is a **triumph**. The deck therefore stops one beat short of a
   victory, **not** one beat short of a reversal. That is the structural inverse of what killed
   `al-haleem@1` rev 2.

**Calibration against shipped precedent.** Bar 5 forbids an arc that *terminates in punishment* just
outside the excerpt. Shipped `al-afuw@1` renders 42:26, whose āyah ends *"the disbelievers will have
a severe punishment"* — **divine, eschatological, and addressed to a category a reader could fear
belonging to** — and the plan records it as non-blocking because it contradicts no beat. Al-Haqq's
neighbourhood is **strictly softer on every axis bar 5 measures**:

| axis | `al-afuw@1` 42:26 (shipped, non-blocking) | `al-haqq@1` 20:71 |
|---|---|---|
| whose punishment | **Allah's** | **a human tyrant's** |
| aimed at whom | a class the reader might be in | the story's **heroes**, whom the reader admires |
| answered? | no | **yes, in the next āyah**, and by a victory |
| register | eschatological | temporal, inside a closed narrative |

A rule cannot forbid the softer case while shipping the harder one. **Bar 5 met.**

**One thing the drafter did not say, which the founder should own in a line.** The strongest form of
the objection is not *"punishment is nearby"* — it is *"the deck sells comfort about recognition
while the passage's next move is that recognition costs these men their limbs."* That exposure is
real on a nightly muḥāsabah surface. It does not fail bar 5 as written, and 20:72–73 answers it
immediately, but it is a judgement the founder should make consciously rather than inherit.

## 3 · Could not verify — the limits of *my own* method

Stated because the founder signs against this document too.

1. **My ḥadīth pass is not independent of sunnah.com as a corpus.** sunnah.com 403s automated
   fetching, so every ḥadīth here was read from a **Wayback capture of the exact bare / lettered
   sunnah.com number**, re-derived through the **CDX API** by me (`/wayback/available` returns
   nothing for archived pages; captures are `content-encoding: zstd` and must be piped through
   `zstd -d`). That is the **same corpus and the same digitisation the drafter used.** It is
   independent at the *fetch* level, not at the *source* level. **No printed edition and no
   Arabic-primary database (Shamela, Dorar, al-Maktaba al-Shāmila) was consulted.**
2. **NO ISNĀD WAS AUDITED.** Published grade lines were accepted at face value — *Da'if (Darussalam)*
   on Tirmidhī 3419, *Sahih (Al-Albani)* on Abū Dāwūd 1353, and the collections' own status for
   Bukhārī and Muslim.
3. **Qurʾān: one API, one translation.** Everything comes from `api.quran.com/api/v4` with
   `translations=20` (Saheeh International). I did not cross-check SI against a second published
   English or a printed muṣḥaf.
4. **⚠️ The weakest link in my confirmation of probe 7.** My ruling that 10:82 sits inside Mūsā's
   speech rests on **SI's punctuation**. The Arabic has no quotation marks and the decision is the

   refusal of 10:82 becomes contestable and the strongest Al-Ḥaqq āyah in the Qurʾān comes back into
   play. Recorded so it is not mistaken for a settled result.
5. **My 24:35 ground-2 judgement (finding C) is my own linguistic assessment**, made without a tafsīr
   corpus. It is the mirror image of the drafter's and carries the same epistemic status — a scholar
   could contest mine too. It does not change the outcome, because ground 1 is sufficient on its own.
6. **The concurrent wave is partially invisible to me as well.** My bar-3 English pass covered the
