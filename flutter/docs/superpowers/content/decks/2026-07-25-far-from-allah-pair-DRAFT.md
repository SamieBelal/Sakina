## Deck `al-wadud@1` — Name₁

**bridge:** You named the distance. This Name is about what happens on the other side of it.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 27, verbatim)*: الْوَدُودُ — Al-Wadud — The Most Loving
**story — The lost camel (Sahih Muslim 2747a / Bukhari 6309):**
> 1. A traveler alone in the desert loses his camel — and with it all his food and water. He lies down in the shade, all hope gone.
> 2. Then he looks up — and it is standing before him. His joy is so wild the words come out backwards: "O Allah, You are my servant and I am Your Lord."
> 3. The Prophet ﷺ said: Allah is more pleased with the repentance of His servant than that man was with his camel.
**verse:** "So seek your Lord's forgiveness and turn to Him in repentance. Surely my Lord is Most Merciful, All-Loving." — Qur'an 11:90 (Wadud in-text)
**dua** *(catalog 27, verbatim)*: اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ حُبَّكَ وَحُبَّ مَنْ يُحِبُّكَ · *Allahumma inni as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuk* · "O Allah, I ask You for Your love and the love of those who love You."
**takeaway (pair synergy):** That pleasure is what the Prophet ﷺ said repentance is met with. And the way back is not yours to invent — Al-Hadi, the second Name of your answer, is the Guide.

**Sources:** [Muslim 2747a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2747a) + [Bukhari 6309](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6309) (both sahih, verified via archive) · [Qur'an 11:90](https://quran.com/11/90) ✅

## Deck `al-hadi@1` — Name₂

**bridge:** Feeling far is one thing. Not knowing the way back is another. This Name is the answer to the second.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 33, verbatim)*: الْهَادِي — Al-Hadi — The Guide
**story — Musa on the road to Midian (Qur'an 28:22):**
> 1. Musa fled Egypt at his lowest — a life taken, the city behind him, nothing ahead but open road.
> 2. He carried no certainty. Only this: "I trust my Lord will guide me to the right way."
> 3. What met him was not the past. It was Midian — shelter, and a family.
**verse** *(excerpt, marked — final clause of a longer verse)*: "…And Allah surely guides the believers to the Straight Path." — Qur'an 22:54 (Name-form هَادِ in-text)
**dua** *(catalog 33, verbatim)*: اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنَا وَاهْدِ بِنَا · *Allahumma ihdina wa ihdi bina* · "O Allah, guide us and make us a means of guidance for others."
**takeaway:** Even the strongest believers ask "guide us" in every prayer, every day. Needing guidance was never falling behind — asking for it is the prayer itself.

**Sources:** [Qur'an 28:22](https://quran.com/28/22) ✅ · beat-1 backstory [28:15](https://quran.com/28/15) + [28:21](https://quran.com/28/21), beat-3 Midian [28:23-27](https://quran.com/28/23) *(⚠️ reference rows added post-review — founder verifies on click)* · [22:54](https://quran.com/22/54) ✅ · [1:6](https://quran.com/1/6) (takeaway basis) ✅
