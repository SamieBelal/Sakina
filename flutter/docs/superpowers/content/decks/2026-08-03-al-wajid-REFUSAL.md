## 1 · The finding in one paragraph

**`w-j-d` occurs 107 times in the Qur'ān (106 verb forms + 1 noun). Of the 106 verbs, only six have
Allah as the grammatical subject — not object, not reported human speech.** Three of those six are
**93:6, 93:7, 93:8** — a single three-āyah cluster in Sūrat aḍ-Ḍuḥā — and **93:6 is already shipped
`al-waliyy@1`'s verse beat**, with that deck's own label stating outright *"the act is Allah's
own"*. The remaining three (7:102, 20:115, 38:44, 51:36 — four, not three; see §2) each carry a
defect serious enough that none clears the five bars cleanly. **Bar 1 is not merely hard here — the
richest possible carrier is already spent by a shipped deck under a different Name, and everything
left over is thin, negative in direction, protagonist-reused, or destruction-adjacent.**

## 2 · Bar 1 — the enumeration

searched for the root `و-ج-د` (all derived forms, including the assimilated imperfect `يجد`/`تجد`/

## 3 · Why 93:6–8 is not available, stated once and not re-derived

**The whole triad shares one grammatical pattern** — `wajadaka [X] fa-[verb]` — repeated three times
across three consecutive āyāt, each pairing a thing Allah found (orphaned, lost, poor) with what He
then did about it (sheltered, guided, enriched). It is the single richest concentration of
Allah-subject `w-j-d` anywhere in the Qur'ān, and **all three verses are now spoken for**:

- **93:6** renders in English on a **shipped** deck's verse beat, explicitly as that deck's bar-1
  carrier — a second deck using it would put the same Qur'ānic sentence, in the same English
  translation, on two different Name screens.
- **93:8** is reserved for Al-Mughni (id 93) by explicit ledger ruling, on grounds independent of
  this Name.
- **93:7**, sandwiched between both, would make Sūrat aḍ-Ḍuḥā (11 āyāt) carry **three** decks
  across **three consecutive** āyāt — a concentration well past the "three decks, not adjacent"
  comfort established for Āl ʿImrān (COLLISION-LEDGER §9ai) or Sūrat Fāṭir. And taking it would still
  render `w-j-d` in English immediately beside `al-waliyy@1`'s own `w-j-d` rendering, one screen
  away in the user's own collected-Names browsing — the *"could a user read both screens and think
  they had been told the same thing twice?"* test (§9o) is failed on the strongest possible grounds:
  it is the **same verb, same grammatical construction, same speaker, same addressee, one āyah
  apart.**

**None of this is a defect in the Name.** It is a scheduling fact created by `al-waliyy@1` reaching
93:6 first — traded there for a legitimate reason (its own Name-root `w-l-y` never appears in Allah's
own act anywhere usable, so it gave up bar 4 entirely and needed a `w-j-d`-carried substitute).

## 4 · The other four — each read and declined

**7:102.** Read in its own passage (7:100–103, about the peoples destroyed before Pharaoh). Allah is
subject of both verbs, but the content is an indictment of "most of them" as *fāsiqīn* (defiantly
disobedient) — teaching this Name through a rebuke of a faithless people inverts the register a
comfort-surface Name needs. Not disqualified by any single bar cleanly, but it would put words in
Allah's mouth condemning "most of them" as the very first thing a user meets under "The Finder" —
a defensible objection, not a rule violation, but heavy enough on its own that it was not chosen.

**20:115.** Allah explicitly **did not find** resolve in Ādam. This is the wrong direction for a
Name whose whole content is *finding* — a deck built on Allah's *failure* to find something would
either (a) have to explain the negation away in authored prose, which is bar-2's exact failure mode
("stated, not shown" via commentary the text does not carry), or (b) present "did not find" as if it
demonstrated "finds," which would misquote the grammar. Also sits at the edge of the Ādam/Fall
narrative — a heavy setting for a hopeful-tone Name.

**38:44.** The strongest candidate on content (Allah affirms Ayyūb's patience — warm, positive,
first-person). Declined because **Ayyūb is already `ash-shafi@1`'s protagonist.** Different āyāt
(38:41–44 vs 21:83–84), different sūrah, different specific moment (affliction-and-affirmation vs
the one-line prayer and its doubled answer) — this is not the "same two āyāt, same framing, same
takeaway" shape that got Ayyūb rejected outright for `ar-raheem@1` R1 (COLLISION-LEDGER §1a). But
reusing a named prophet a second time, this early in the catalogue (24 of 99 Names shipped/drafted,
75 remaining), spends protagonist-ground the project has elsewhere treated as a real cost — id 1's
own refusal named "Mūsā a **three-deck** figure" as part of why that Name could not be built this
wave. Ayyūb becoming a two-deck figure is a smaller version of the same concern, and this deck did
not need to spend it to make its case, because no version of this deck's case is otherwise made.

**51:36.** Allah-subject, but the setting is **the destruction of Sodom** — "We found not therein
except one house of Muslims" is a headcount taken immediately before a city is destroyed. Bar 5's
concern (a comfort-Name deck must not sit on or beside a punishment passage) is not "just outside the
excerpt" here — the punishment **is the excerpt's own subject**, one clause away. Declined on bar 5,
cleanly.

   specifically because it opened for a different one first.**
2. **A hadith route**, if one exists and can be fetched. This refusal covers the Qur'ān exhaustively
   (the higher-authority source, per plan §5's "Qur'ān-sourced narratives outrank hadith when impact
   is comparable") but **not** the hadith corpus — `sunnah.com`'s search index 403s automated
   fetching (confirmed this pass) and no specific candidate hadith number using `w-j-d` with Allah as
   an explicit finder-subject is known to this drafter to check via Wayback CDX. Catalogue id 71's
   own `hadith` field is **empty** — no card-level lead exists either. **A drafter with a specific
   citable number to check might find something this pass could not**, but composing a guess to then
   "verify" would be exactly the fabrication risk this pipeline exists to prevent, so none was made.
3. **A founder decision to accept 7:102, 20:115 or 51:36 on the reasoning above**, overruling the
   register/setting objections stated. None of the three fails a bar outright; all three were
   declined on a judgment call this drafter is not authorized to make unilaterally, given the
   project's standing rule that reasoning-based rejections are exactly what an adversarial or founder
   pass exists to test, not something a drafter self-certifies.
