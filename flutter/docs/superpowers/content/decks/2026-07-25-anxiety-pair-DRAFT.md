## Deck `as-salam@1` — Name₁ of chip `anxiety`

**Beat 1 · bridge:**
> For the weight you named — a mind that won't stop racing — there is a Name.

**Beat 2 · name_intro** *(from `collectible_names.json` id 6, verbatim)*:
> السَّلَامُ — As-Salam — The Source of Serenity

*✅ DECIDED (founder, 2026-07-25): "The Source of Serenity" adopted app-wide per quran.com 59:23 — catalog + all four code render-sites updated the same day; intro stays catalog-verbatim.*

**Beats 3-5 · story — The Cave:**
> 1. Hunted out of Mecca, the Prophet ﷺ hid in a cave with his closest friend. The searchers came so close that Abu Bakr said: if one of them looks down, he will see us. The Prophet ﷺ was unshaken.
> 2. He answered: **"What do you think of two, the third of whom is Allah? Do not worry — Allah is certainly with us."**
> 3. And Allah sent down His serenity upon him. The danger was still outside — the stillness inside was real.

**Beat 6 · verse:**
> "Surely in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find comfort." — Qur'an 13:28

**Beat 7 · dua** *(catalog id 6, verbatim in full — these are the Prophet's ﷺ own post-prayer words, Sahih Muslim 591)*:
> اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
> *Allahumma Antas-Salam wa minkas-salam tabarakta ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram*
> "O Allah, You are Peace and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Owner of Majesty and Honor."

**Beat 8 · takeaway (pair-synergy beat — Name₁ deck carries it):**
> As-Salam — peace itself — is the Name for what is inside you. Al-Wakeel, the second Name of your answer, is the One you trust with what is outside your hands.

## Deck `al-wakeel@1` — Name₂ of chip `anxiety`

**Beat 1 · bridge:**
> A racing mind spends the night rehearsing tomorrow. This is the Name of the One you can hand tomorrow to.

**Beat 2 · name_intro** *(catalog id 35, verbatim + one authored gloss line to unify the renderings the user will meet)*:
> الْوَكِيلُ — Al-Wakeel — The Trustee
> *the Guardian you hand your affairs to*

**Beats 3-5 · story — After Uhud:**
> 1. Wounded after Uhud, the believers were warned that their enemies had gathered against them — and were told to be afraid. They were not.
> 2. The warning only made them grow stronger in faith. They answered with one sentence: **ḥasbunallāhu wa niʿmal-wakīl** — "Allah alone is sufficient for us, and He is the best Protector."
> 3. "So they returned with Allah's favours and grace, suffering no harm." The harm they had been told to fear never came.

**Beat 6 · verse** *(excerpt, marked as such)*:
> "…And whoever puts their trust in Allah, then He ˹alone˺ is sufficient for them." — Qur'an 65:3

**Beat 7 · dua** *(catalog id 35, verbatim — the same words the believers spoke at Uhud, and Ibrahim at the fire)*:
> حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ
> *Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal-Wakil*
> "Allah is enough for us, and he is the best disposer of affairs."

**Beat 8 · takeaway:**
> You were never asked to hold every outcome. Handing them over is not giving up — it is trust, placed with the One who holds them.
