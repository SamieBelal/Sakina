## 1 · What changed, and why it unblocks the Name

**R1's error, named exactly:** every candidate was scored as a single text that had to simultaneously
*show* non-need (bar 1/2) and *carry* `غَنِيّ` (bar 4). No such text exists cleanly. **It never had
to be one text.** `al-wahid@1`'s story (21:21–22) carries no `و-ح-د` at all — the root is recovered on
an independent verse beat (16:51). `al-muhyi@1`'s story (30:48–50) is a no-protagonist parable; its
root-bearing clause is corroborated by a *second*, independent citation (41:39). Applying the same
shape here:

- **Story (bar 1 + bar 2): a clause of Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2577a that R1 never used** — different from the
  clause `al-quddus@1` (shipped) already spent. Allah's own first-person words; a demonstrated
  incapacity, not a stated adjective.
- **Verse (bar 4 + bar 3/5 register): Qurʾān 35:15–17** — carries `الْغَنِيُّ` explicitly, and (per
  the coordinator's own reading, adopted below) is not an accusation but a permanent, universal
  condition named for the reader on their best day as much as their worst.

**Second change: counterfactual-as-demonstration is now precedented.** `al-wahid@1`'s entire bar-1
case is the grammatical move *"had there been X, Y would have happened — and Y never happened."*
35:16's *"If He wills, He can do away with you and bring forth a new creation"* is the same move. R1
treated it as merely "demonstrated but risky"; it is now treated as **demonstrated, full stop**, with
the register question (not the bar-1/2 question) as what remains to weigh.

## 3 · The story — Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2577a, the harm/benefit clause (not al-quddus@1's clause)

**Checked, as instructed, rather than assumed.** Muslim 2577a is a single long ḥadīth qudsī with nine
`يَا عِبَادِي` clauses. `al-quddus@1` (shipped) spends clauses 7–8 (piety/wickedness → *"would not
increase/decrease My dominion"*) as its entire story, beats 3–4. **Clause 6 — the harm/benefit
clause, immediately before those two — is unspent by any deck in the current 45-deck asset**, checked
by re-fetching the page directly:

> Arabic (verified live, `web.archive.org/web/20260801213232id_/https://sunnah.com/muslim:2577a`):
> `يَا عِبَادِي إِنَّكُمْ لَنْ تَبْلُغُوا ضَرِّي فَتَضُرُّونِي وَلَنْ تَبْلُغُوا نَفْعِي
> فَتَنْفَعُونِي`
> Page English: *"O My servants, you will not attain harming Me so as to harm Me, and will not
> attain benefitting Me so as to benefit Me."*

**Why this is a different claim from al-quddus@1's, not the same claim from a different verse.**
Al-Quddus's clauses (7–8) are about the *moral quality* of deeds (piety vs. wickedness) not moving
*a measure of standing* (`مُلْكِي`, "My dominion"). This clause is about *any act at all, in either
direction* (harm or benefit) never *arriving* — not a claim about magnitude under a moral spectrum,
but a claim about reach: nothing you do, good or bad, is capable of touching Him. That is
Al-Ghaniyy's own content — non-need as a structural fact, not a claim about how impressive or
unimpressive your record is. Full comparison in §6.

**Narrator: Abū Dharr** (`عَنِ أَبِي ذَرٍّ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ فِيمَا رَوَى عَنِ اللَّهِ`) — not Abū
Idrīs al-Khawlānī, the sub-narrator `al-quddus@1` names for the kneeling detail. This deck names Abū
Dharr and does **not** use the kneeling detail (deliberately — it is `al-quddus@1`'s own rendered
beat, see bar-3 sweep below).

### Register — the coordinator's ruling, adopted and checked against the text

`أَنتُمُ ٱلْفُقَرَآءُ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ` (*"you are those in need of Allāh"*) is second-person plural,
addressed to `يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ` — **all mankind**, not a rebuked party singled out by a
preceding clause. Checked against the immediate predecessor: **35:14 is about idols not hearing or
answering prayer**, not about the reader's own disbelief; the pronoun shift at 35:15 is a fresh
address, not a continuation of a rebuke. The condition named (*"in need of Allāh"*) is unconditionally
true of the reader on their best night as much as their worst — it is not contingent on having
disbelieved or withheld anything, unlike every member of R1's Cluster C. **This is the distinction
that separates 35:15–17 from 6:133 (still sitting inside a Fire/Judgment-Day-adjacent passage) and
from 47:38/57:24/60:6/64:6 (explicit rebukes to those who turn away or withhold).**

### 7c · The move — against `al-quddus@1` specifically, the closest deck in the corpus

**This is the row a verifier will attack first**, more than the al-Mughni row R1 already closed.

> **`al-quddus@1` beat 7 (shipped, from the SAME long ḥadīth, clauses 7–8):** *"Nothing anyone has
> ever done has made Him more. Nothing anyone has ever done has made Him less. Whatever you are
> carrying tonight has not changed Him — and what does get through was never the offering. It is the
> piety from you."*
> **`al-ghaniyy@1` beat 8 (this draft, from clause 6 of the same ḥadīth + Qurʾān 35:15–17):** *"There
> was never a debt. Not one your best days paid down, not one your worst days added to. And knowing
> that — knowing He loses nothing whichever way today went — He still says: ask anyway."*

**Zero shared 3-grams. Both land on *your actions do not move Him either way*.** This is precisely
§9aq's worked example shape — a collision in the move survivable by every mechanical string check.
**Reasons offered for separability, not a ruling:**

1. **The axis differs.** `al-quddus@1`'s clauses (7–8) are about *magnitude under a moral spectrum* —
   piety vs. wickedness, weighed against *"My dominion"* (a measure of Him). This deck's clause (6) is
   about *reach* — whether any act, in either direction, is even capable of arriving at Him at all.
   One is a claim about scale; the other is a claim about contact.
2. **The turn differs.** `al-quddus@1` resolves outward — *"what does get through… is the piety from
   you"* (an answer about what Allah *does* receive). This deck resolves toward the duʿā — *"He still
   says: ask anyway"* (an answer about what Allah still *invites*, despite receiving nothing that
   counts as a debt paid or incurred). One closes on the shape of an offering; the other closes on an
   invitation that does not require one.
3. **Vocabulary hygiene, measured:** *"dominion"*, *"piety"*, *"offering"*, *"more/less"* — all n=0 in
   this deck. *"Debt"*, *"paid down"*, *"ask anyway"* — all n=0 in `al-quddus@1`.
4. **The narration overlap is real and disclosed, not hidden** (§3). Both decks draw from Muslim

## 8 · Al-Mughni separation — restated from R1, unaffected by the carrier/story split

**Unchanged, and the coordinator adopted it into the ledger without argument.** Al-Mughni (93) is
