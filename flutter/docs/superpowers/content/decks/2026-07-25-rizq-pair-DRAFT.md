## Deck `ar-razzaq@1` — Name₁

**bridge:** For the weight you named — the fear of not having enough, of not providing — there is a Name.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 13, verbatim)*: الرَّزَّاقُ — Ar-Razzaq — The Provider
**story — The birds (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2344, hasan):**
> 1. The Prophet ﷺ answered the fear of tomorrow with a picture: a bird, going out in the morning with nothing in hand.
> 2. He said: if you relied upon Allah as He deserves to be relied upon, He would provide for you as He provides for the birds — they go out in the morning empty, and return full.
> 3. And the bird still goes out — reliance was never sitting still; it is going out with the weight handed over.
**verse** *(composite of 65:2-3, disclosed)*: "And whoever is mindful of Allah, He will make a way out for them, and provide for them from sources they could never imagine." — Qur'an 65:2-3
**dua** *(catalog 13, verbatim)*: اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ · *Allahumma ikfini bi halalika 'an haramik wa aghnini bi fadlika amman siwak* · "O Allah, suffice me with what is lawful against what is unlawful, and enrich me by Your favor over all others."
**takeaway (pair synergy):** Ar-Razzaq is about what reaches you. Al-Fattah — the second Name of your answer — is about the doors it comes through.

## Deck `al-fattah@1` — Name₂

**bridge:** And for the doors that look shut — the application, the job, the way forward — this is the Name of the One who opens.
**name_intro** *(catalog id 23, verbatim)*: الْفَتَّاحُ — Al-Fattah — The Opener
**story — Hudaybiyyah: the shut door that was a victory (Qur'an 48:1 + Bukhari 4172/4833):**
> 1. At Hudaybiyyah the Prophet ﷺ turned back from the Ka'bah, short of the pilgrimage they had set out for. Every door looked shut.
> 2. On the road home, revelation named that very moment: "Indeed, We have granted you a clear triumph."
> 3. The shut door itself was the opening — named a triumph before anyone could yet see why.
**verse** *(excerpt, marked)*: "Whatever mercy Allah opens up for people, none can withhold it." — Qur'an 35:2
**dua** *(catalog 23, verbatim)*: يَا فَتَّاحُ افْتَحْ لَنَا خَيْرَ الْفَتْحِ · *Ya Fattahu iftah lana khayral fath* · "O Opener, open for us the best of openings."
**takeaway:** None of the gatekeepers you fear — the interviewer, the landlord, the market — can withhold what He opens. That is what this Name means.

**Sources:** [Qur'an 48:1](https://quran.com/48/1) ✅ · [Bukhari 4833](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4833) (revelation moment) + [Bukhari 4172](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4172) ("It refers to the Al-Hudaibiya Pledge") — both sahih, verified via archive · beat-1's turned-back-short-of-the-pilgrimage framing is uncontested sirah documented in Bukhari's Hudaybiyyah chapters (e.g. [2731-2732](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2731)), beyond the two cited hadiths (audit note 2026-07-25) · [Qur'an 35:2](https://quran.com/35/2) ✅
