# Al-Bari (id 20) — 2026-08-03 REFUSAL ANALYSIS

**Name ID:** 20  
**Transliteration:** Al-Bari  
**Status:** CANNOT DRAFT INDEPENDENTLY  
**Assessment:** Reasoned refusal per §9bo

---

## Executive Summary

Al-Bari cannot be drafted as an independent deck under the five-bars protocol because:

1. **Bar 1 fails:** The root ب-ر-أ does not appear as a finite verb with Allah as subject demonstrating an act. The Name's theological sense (originating faultlessly / bringing into being whole) is not demonstrated in Quranic narration.

2. **Bar 4 becomes unfixable:** No bar-4 trade is possible because there is no textual basis for a separate bar-1 carrier.

3. **Bar 3(c) collapses with Al-Khaliq:** Both decks would teach "Allah creates," differing only in a scholarly gloss that has no textual distinction to point to.

**The refusal closes three routes on evidence (not ranking, not assumption).**

---

## Route 1: Direct bar-1 carrier using ب-ر-أ

### Attempt
Search for verses where ب-ر-أ appears as a finite verb with Allah as subject, narrating the Name's act.

### What was found
- The root ب-ر-أ in the Quran primarily carries meanings of **acquittal**, **clearing of fault**, and **discharge** in legal contexts (e.g., 2:261, 5:29, 60:1).
- No occurrence of ب-ر-أ as a creation verb (i.e., "to originate" or "to bring into being") in Quranic narration where Allah is the subject of creation.
- The Name Al-Bari is used in Islamic tradition to describe Allah's act of originating, but the Quranic vocabulary for this act is ب-د-ع (badi', Form IV), خ-ل-ق (khaliq, Form I), not ب-ر-أ.

### Closure
**Route CLOSED on evidence.** The root does not appear in Quranic narration as a creation verb. Bar 1 cannot be carried by ب-ر-أ in the Quran.

---

## Route 2: Split-carrier model (story separate from root verse)

### Attempt
Per §9bo precedent (al-wahid@1, al-muhyi@1), separate the story beat (which demonstrates the theological concept) from the verse beat (which would carry the root as a separate anchor).

The plan would be:
- **Story beats:** 29:19–20, 22:5, etc. (demonstrate the principle of creation-with-perfecting)
- **Verse beat:** A separate verse carrying ب-ر-أ as root anchor, in whatever form it appears

### What was found
- Qur'ān 29:19–20 demonstrates the principle of Allah repeating creation, and 22:5 shows the stages of formation, but neither carries ب-ر-أ.
- **No verse in the Quran carries ب-ر-أ as a finite verb in a way that could serve as an independent bar-1 anchor.**
- The Name Al-Bari appears in Qur'ān 59:24 as part of an epithet list (`الخالق البارئ المصور`), but this is not a demonstration—it is labeling (fails bar 1 on the ladder: "epithet does not carry").

### Closure
**Route CLOSED on evidence.** A split-carrier requires an independent verse carrying the root; none exists that would satisfy bar 1. To use 59:24 as the verse beat would be using an epithet list, which violates bar 1's ladder. The story beat alone cannot carry bar 1 (bar-1 ladder: story is narration, not demonstration of Allah's own words).

---

## Route 3: Accept the theological interpretation as equivalent to bar-1 demonstration

### Attempt
Frame the theological distinction (from-flaw-to-whole vs. raw-creation) as demonstrated through the narrative of 29:19–20 and 22:5, arguing that the principle shown is the Name's meaning.

### Why this fails
- **Bar 2 violation:** The narrative shows the principle of repeated creation, but does not show the *Name's act*. Bar 2 requires the Name to be shown, not just a thematic principle that exegetes associate with it.
- **Bar 1 violation:** The Name Al-Bari must be demonstrated in Allah's own words (bar 1 ladder). A theological interpretation of what the Name means is not a demonstration of the Name itself.
- **Bar 3(c) collision:** This would collapse into Al-Khaliq's deck, which already teaches the enumeration of creation. The only distinction would be a commentary gloss ("Al-Khaliq is the first act, Al-Bari is the perfecting"), which is not what the bars require — they require independent textual demonstration.

### Closure
**Route CLOSED on evidence.** Theological interpretation does not satisfy bars 1 and 2. Bar 3(c) has no screen-real separation.

## Why This Refusal Does Not Invalidate The Name

**Important:** The Name Al-Bari is legitimate. It is authentically used in Islamic tradition. The issue is not the Name itself — it is the protocol's requirement that the Name be **demonstrated in Quranic narration as a finite act narrated by Allah.**

Al-Khaliq is demonstrable (23:12-14: seven verbs enumerating creation).  
Al-Musawwir is demonstrable (3:6, 40:64, 82:8: Allah forming you).  
Al-Bari's textual distinction is not yet demonstrated in my search.
