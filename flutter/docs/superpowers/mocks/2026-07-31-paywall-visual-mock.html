Third pass — the card, and page 2's dead middle

  • The card is the Silver card now. Page 1 previously drew an emerald card labelled "Silver", which is two errors at once. The onboarding reveal awards a deterministic Silver tier (queue_plan_screen.dart:76), and a Silver card in this app is a cool dark slate ground (#353847 → #2A2D3A) with silver metallics and a gold medallion ring — palette and structure taken from silver_mini_ornate_card.dart. The paywall must show the exact artifact the user just watched land, or the two surfaces contradict each other on the page where trust is being asked for.
  • Page 2 spans instead of stacking. The three moments now distribute down the full height, the thread between them runs emerald → gold → resolved grey, and "No charge today." moved down beside the CTA where a reassurance belongs. Nothing was added. The empty middle wasn't a content shortage — it was the page failing to say the one thing it exists to say, which is that seven days is a stretch of time. A long thread says that without a word of new copy, and the alternative (filling it with more selling) is exactly what this page's transparency job forbids.
  • Day labels stepped up to 20px so "Today / Day 6 / Day 7" carry the page, with descriptions capped at ~30 characters per line so they read as beats rather than paragraphs.

Second pass — house rules

  • Killed the CTA drop shadow. DESIGN.md §4: "There are no drop shadows in the theme. Separation is border + background only." The button now sits flat like every other elevated button in the app. The only remaining shadow is on the revealed card, tinted to the emerald it sits on — that one is an illustrated artifact, not chrome.
  • Radii corrected to the system: plan card 14, buttons 12, icon chips tighter at 10. The first pass used 16–18 throughout, which read soft in a way nothing else in the app does.
  • Titles moved to displayLarge — 34/700/−0.02em, the documented standard for main screen titles. They were 29/600, which is why the pages felt quieter than the rest of the app.
  • Gold is fills only. The dismissal ornament now strokes in goldInk #9A6F37; bright gold at ~2.2:1 on cream fails contrast for anything that carries meaning.
  • Copy drift reverted. The first pass shouted the badge as "7 DAYS FREE FIRST" and the tier as "SILVER" — the approved copy is sentence case, and copy is locked.
  • The benefit row is the shipped one — 22px circle, primaryLight fill, 14px check, 10px gap, bodyMedium at 1.35. Lifted from paywall_screen.dart:852 so the two surfaces can't drift.
  • Motion added: staggered fade + 9px rise, 450ms easeOutCubic — the same beat-advance rhythm as the sacred canvas. Hover and pressed states on everything tappable; honours prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Store economics are illustrative in this historical mock: the shipped renderer now reads localised annual and weekly prices from RevenueCat, derives any savings label from those packages, and renders no economics when offerings are unavailable.
  • Page 3 scrolls with a soft bottom mask, CTA pinned — the 844pt frame can't hold title + five benefits + two plans + terms + legal, and a hidden Restore button is an App Store review risk.
One thing I did not add. High-converting paywalls almost always carry social proof at the decision moment — a rating, a count, a line from a review. Sakina's copy firewall doesn't forbid it, but the approved draft has none and the reverence posture makes "join 12,000 believers" the wrong register. If you want it, the honest version is a single App Store rating line above the plan cards, and it needs your call before I add it — it changes locked copy.
