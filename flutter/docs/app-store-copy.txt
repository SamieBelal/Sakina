DESCRIPTION (4,000 char limit, plain text only, no markdown)
================================================================================

Tell Sakina how you feel. In return, discover the Name of Allah for this moment — with Quran verses and a dua chosen just for you.

Sakina is a daily devotional for Muslims who want to bring iman into the small, real moments of life. The anxious commute. The grateful morning. The quiet night you can't sleep.

How it works:
• Open the app, write or tap how you're feeling
• Sakina matches your feeling to one of the 99 Names of Allah
• Read the verses, recite the dua, save it to your journal
• Return tomorrow. Your streak grows. Your heart settles.

What's inside:

THE 99 NAMES OF ALLAH
Beautifully presented Arabic calligraphy, transliteration, and meaning. Discover a new Name through your daily check-in, or browse the full collection.

DAILY MUHASABAH
A gentle four-question check-in that helps you notice what's really happening inside — and meets you with scripture for it.

PERSONAL DUAS
Tell Sakina what you need help with and receive a dua in Arabic, transliteration, and English. Built around your moment, grounded in tradition.

REFLECT WITH SCRIPTURE
Write freely about what's on your heart. Sakina returns a Name of Allah, verses from the Quran, and a related dua — never invented, always sourced from verified Islamic scholarship.

YOUR JOURNAL
Every check-in, every reflection, every saved dua — kept in a beautiful journal you can return to. Watch your iman grow over weeks and months.

NAME COLLECTION
Build your collection of the 99 Names through daily practice. Each Name unlocks beautiful collectible cards as you deepen your connection.

GENTLE STREAKS
A daily flame keeps you returning. Miss a day? Use a Streak Freeze. This is a habit, not a guilt trip.

WHY SAKINA IS DIFFERENT

Sakina never generates Quran verses, hadith, or religious teachings. Every verse and every Name comes from a pre-verified scholarly database. The technology only helps connect your feeling to the right scripture — the scripture itself is exactly what Muslims have read for fourteen centuries.

Designed with care. Arabic typography that honors the language. Soft, warm interface that feels like opening a beautiful mushaf. No ads. No noise.

PREMIUM
Try Sakina Premium free for 3 days. Unlimited Reflect sessions, unlimited personal duas, full access to the daily Discover Name feature, and the complete card collection. Cancel anytime.

Made by Muslims, for Muslims. Whether you're returning to your deen, deepening your daily practice, or just looking for one quiet moment with Allah each day — Sakina is here for you.

Indeed, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest. (Quran 13:28)

ANSWER: Yes (HTTPS for Supabase, OpenAI, RevenueCat, OneSignal,

FOLLOW-UP: "Does your app qualify for any of the exemptions provided

ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false in Info.plist to avoid being
asked on every build.
