# What Muslims Struggle With — Research Basis for the Onboarding Chip Taxonomy

**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Purpose:** validate/amend the ~6-chip "what's weighing on you" taxonomy (One Ship, decision ① in [`../superpowers/plans/2026-07-23-conversion-refactor-changes-and-implementation.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-07-23-conversion-refactor-changes-and-implementation.md) Phase 0.1). Two independent web-research passes: evidence side (surveys/clinical) and demand side (what Muslims actively seek help for).

## 1. Evidence side — prevalence in the reel demographic

Anchor source: **Muslim Youth Helpline, "Muslim Youth: What's the Issue?" 2019** — N=1,077 UK Muslims aged 16-30, recruited via social media (i.e., the IG/TikTok Islamic-content audience). Ranked "have you suffered from…" table (N=1,064): [PDF](https://myh.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/MYH-Research-Report-Muslim-Youth-Whats-the-Issue-1-2.pdf), appendix p.15.

| Rank | Struggle | Prevalence / evidence | Muslim-shaped? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anxiety / worry about the future | **63%** (70% women); #1 at [Naseeha helpline](https://www.naseeha.org/helpline-textline) | Universal |
| 2 | Low mood / depression / hopelessness | **52%**; 32% suicidal thoughts; US Muslims ~2× suicide-attempt odds ([ISPU/JAMA](https://ispu.org/suicide-attempts-of-muslims/)) | Universal + a Muslim despair-of-mercy layer ([Yaqeen](https://yaqeeninstitute.org/read/paper/repentance-as-a-way-of-life-islam-spirituality-practice)) |
| 3 | Family expectations / conflict | **47%** (53% women) | Culturally weighted |
| 4 | Religious issues: iman low, practice inconsistency, guilt | **30% — UNPROMPTED** (out-ranked money/employment) | **Uniquely Muslim** |
| 5 | Doubt / iman dips | Widespread; driven by moral/social not theological concerns; **no age effect** ([Yaqeen doubt study](https://yaqeeninstitute.org/read/paper/what-causes-muslims-to-doubt-islam-a-quantitative-analysis)) | **Uniquely Muslim** |
| 6 | Waswas / religious OCD | 10% OCD self-report; religious themes in up to ~52% of OCD in Muslim samples ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22304890/), [Khalil Center](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/329588956_Islamically_Integrated_Treatment_of_Obsessive-Compulsive_Disorder_Scrupulosity_Waswasa_in_Muslim_Patients)) | **Uniquely Muslim** |
| 7 | Sin-shame cycles (porn, young men) | **31% of men** self-report porn addiction; 70% of Muslim viewers are practicing AND believe it immoral; 53% won't seek help ([FYI](https://thefyi.org/pornography-muslim-youth-preliminary-research-findings/)) | Muslim-shaped guilt dynamics |
| 8 | Identity / discrimination | 25% identity struggles; 48% of US Muslims experienced discrimination ([Pew 2017](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2017/07/26/findings-from-pew-research-centers-2017-survey-of-us-muslims/)) | Muslim-shaped in the West |
| 9 | Loneliness / marriage pressure / silent suffering | 26% relationship stress; **40.2% of men told NOBODY about their last issue** (MYH p.13) | Culturally shaped |
| 10 | Money / studies / work (rizq) | Exams 58% (16-22s); employment 21%, money 18% (rises with age) | Universal, Islamically re-framable |

## 2. Demand side — what Muslims actively seek help for

- **Anxiety** — #1 across every channel: dua taxonomies ([SunnahWise](https://sunnahwise.com/duas/), Hisn al-Muslim's canonical "Anxiety & distress" category), Sabr's flagship "7 Days of Anxiety" course, and the acquisition channel itself.
- **Repeated sin → guilt → repentance** — the #1 emotional ask-a-scholar genre ([IslamQA repentance tree](https://islamqa.info/en/categories/topics/217/repentance)); recurring phrasing: "I sin, repent, sin again — am I a hypocrite?" Shame is the core, not fiqh.
- **Waswas** — an entire SeekersGuidance answer genre (faith/purity/marriage-validity misgivings).
- **Hardship / "tested" / "why me"** — the single biggest viral reel framing (["Allah Will Not Burden A Soul"](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/allah-will-not-burden-a-soul), "signs Allah is guiding you") — i.e., the Reel-2/sign-contract arrival state.
- **Marriage / loneliness in singleness** — a whole dua-content industry; converts' dominant struggles are isolation + family estrangement ([BBC](https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/articles/c20109wwwvqo)).
- **Rizq / money / debt** — top-level category in every dua taxonomy.
- **Far from Allah / can't stay consistent** — recurring confessional genre; **IslamQA's largest live category is Psychological & Social Issues** (~2× any other); Hallow's top intake goals ("build a prayer habit," "grow closer to God") are the Christian mirror.
- Bench signals: grief, sleep, exams, parents (devotional, not a "weighing on me" problem).

**Competitor intakes found:** Hallow — build a prayer habit / sleep better / reduce stress & anxiety / grow closer to God / other. Sabr — anxiety, hope, stress, sleep, study + mood journal. Hisn al-Muslim canon — morning/evening, anxiety & distress, forgiveness, protection, etc. **No Muslim competitor offers a sin-guilt or iman-low intake option — that's the open differentiator.**

## 3. Resulting taxonomy (the amended draft in Phase 0.1)

Chips 1-3, 5-6 confirmed (anxiety · heavy/hopeless · guilt/return · rizq · lonely/unseen); **"lost/no direction" REPLACED by "I feel far from Allah" (Al-Wadud + Al-Hadi)** — bottom-ranked on both research sides vs. 30%-unprompted + zero-competitor-analogue for iman-low. Sign chip unchanged (Ar-Rahman + Al-Latif) and doubles as the "tested/hardship" viral-framing catcher. Bench: family strain (47% — strongest cut), grief, sleep.

**Binding design notes:** never name a chip "waswas" and never build mechanics inviting same-day repeated mood→verse re-checks (Khalil Center: reassurance loops ARE the pathology; the 1/day reveal cap is protective). Any future doubt chip is experiential ("faith feels shaky"), never intellectual. Guilt-chip decks lead with mercy — despair-of-mercy is the named pathology to avoid deepening. Free-text keyword map catches family/marriage/exam/sleep/grief terms → nearest chip.
